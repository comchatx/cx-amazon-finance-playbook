## What this covers

**Scope.** Money that flows back across a sale after it posts: customer refunds (FBA and seller-fulfilled), what of a refund is taken from the seller and what is given back, returnless and partial refunds, restocking fees, prepaid return labels charged to the seller, replacements; A-to-z Guarantee claims, chargebacks and SAFE-T claims as debits and credits; FBA inventory reimbursements (what Amazon pays without a claim, how value is computed, the claim windows as dated facts, reversals, cash vs inventory); and the "adjustment" lines of Finances and the settlement report. Each is tied to its identifiers (order id, adjustment item id, reimbursement id, case id, FNSKU) and to the file or operation that records it. Also (gap fill, 2026-11-10) the value a returned or unsellable unit brings back through FBA Grade and Resell, FBA Donations and Amazon Resale (§ J); liquidation as money is in `fulfilment-charges.md`. Claims as a job — finding what Amazon owes and filing — belong to a later plugin; this file records what that plugin would add (§ What only Seller Central shows, P-ref-30).

**Skills it would feed.** The pack's profit/earnings skill (a refund lowers a product's earnings by more than its principal; a reimbursement raises them, usually not to the sale value); a settlement/payout reading skill (which settlement and Finances lines are refunds, claims, reimbursements and reserves, and how they join to orders); and a shared `references/` file on refund and reimbursement money (column meanings, the identifier joins, the dated windows). The stock playbook (brief 307) owns the inventory ledger and the stock states; this file uses the ledger only where money needs it.

**Why a senior operator cares.** On a large FBA account, refunds are the second-largest line after fees, and they are not the mirror of a sale: the principal, tax and promotion are reversed, the referral fee comes back less an administration fee, and the FBA fulfilment fee is gone. Reimbursements arrive weeks or months after the loss, under a different id, on a different settlement, at a value Amazon now sets from the seller's sourcing cost, and can be reversed later. A-to-z claims and service chargebacks are funded from the seller's balance. Anyone who books "net sales minus refunds" without these rules misstates margin per SKU, and anyone who does not track the windows below loses money that can no longer be claimed.

## Amazon's own rules

### A. Customer refunds: what moves, who triggers it, when

**A1. What a refund reverses, in the model's words.** A refund is carried in Finances v0 as a `ShipmentEvent`-shaped item under `RefundEventList` ("A shipment, refund, guarantee claim, or chargeback"). The refund-only fields are `ItemChargeAdjustmentList` ("A list of charge adjustments associated with the shipment item. This value is only returned for refunds, guarantee claims, and chargeback events."), `ItemFeeAdjustmentList`, `PromotionAdjustmentList`, `CostOfPointsReturned`, and `OrderAdjustmentItemId` ("An Amazon-defined order adjustment identifier defined for refunds, guarantee claims, and chargeback events"). The charge types a refund can carry are the `ChargeComponent` values, including `Principal`, `Tax`, `MarketplaceFacilitatorTax-Principal/-Shipping/-Giftwrap/-Other` ("The tax that is withheld on…"), `Discount`, `ShippingCharge`, `ShippingTax`, `Giftwrap`, `GiftwrapTax`, `Goodwill` ("The amount of money that is given to a buyer as a gesture of goodwill or to compensate for pain and suffering in the buying experience"), `RestockingFee` ("The charge that is applied to the buyer when returning a product in certain categories"), `ReturnShipping` ("The amount of money that is given to the buyer to compensate for shipping the item back if we are at fault"), `PointsFee`, `GenericDeduction` ("A generic bad debt deduction"), `FreeReplacementReturnShipping` ("The compensation for return shipping when a buyer receives the wrong item, requests a free replacement, and returns the incorrect item"), `PaymentMethodFee`, `ExportCharge`, `SAFE-TReimbursement`, the India `TCS-*` family and `PaidthroughEBT`. `FeeType` has no closed list; it points to the fee schedule and "FBA features, services and fees". [official] [command-verified] (`models/finances-api-model/financesV0.json`, definitions `ShipmentEvent`, `ShipmentItem`, `ChargeComponent`, `FeeComponent`; read 2026-11-09.)

**A2. The referral fee comes back less a refund administration fee.** US: on a refund Amazon returns the referral fee "minus the applicable refund administration fee, which is the lesser of $5.00 or 20% of the applicable referral fee". [official] (Selling on Amazon fee schedule, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200336920, version 119, read 2026-10-14 by the listing seat, `research/angles/price-position.md` §H.) UK wording, relayed by a seller from the help page: "When you create a full refund for a non-BMVD product, Amazon retains 20% of the original order-related fees, up to a maximum of £5, for each line item in the refund. This amount is held back as a refund administrative fee." "When you create a partial refund for a non-BMVD product, Amazon retains 20% of the original order-related fees, up to a maximum of £5, for each line item in the refund and credits you with a proportional amount of the Referral Fee originally charged for the order based on the percentage of the item price refunded to the buyer." "If you refund two different items in an order, Amazon retains up to £5 for each item, for a maximum of £10". BMVD is "Books, Music, Video and DVD" (separate rules). [official, relayed] (Seller Forums EU, "Refund Cost", reply by Seller_zKEt2RiaID5EU quoting the help page, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/3e43da0c-1b96-473b-9898-fe57db2e48bb, read 2026-11-09.) UAE: a seller quotes the same rule "up to a maximum of AED 15, for each line item in the refund" (https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/22c4dcb2-808c-4dd5-ae10-f914306c57b9, read 2026-11-09) [official, relayed]. The cap's currency and amount differ per store; the base differs in wording (US "applicable referral fee", UK "original order-related fees").

**A3. The FBA fulfilment fee is not refunded on a customer refund.** No Amazon page read states this in so many words. Sellers state it in forum threads: in 22c4dcb2 (2024-11-04) a UAE seller writes that on a re-sold return "amazon again will charge the same fulfillment fee"; in f29481bb (UK, 2024-03-08) a seller asks whether the gap on a refund "is … the FBA fee waivered plus an % admin fee". [opinion] (The earlier quote "any fees related to fulfillment of the order by Amazon will not be refunded, however you will be credited the final value fees" is a paraphrase: exact wording not found on d8f86449, 22c4dcb2 or f29481bb, 2026-11-10; b2b31952 is cited only by a partial id and was not re-read.) The model does not settle it either way: `FeeType` has no closed list (A1), so the model neither lists nor excludes a fulfilment-fee line in `ItemFeeAdjustmentList` [command-verified]. A refund without a return also leaves the unit with the buyer. A3 is a practitioner fact with an official gap; see Open questions.

**A4. Who can trigger a refund on a seller-fulfilled order.** Amazon lists the cases in which it "automatically issues refunds for returns": "Refund at first scan: … eligible requests that are authorized automatically through our Prepaid Return Label program are refunded automatically once the buyer drops off the return at a designated carrier location." "Returnless refunds: We'll issue returnless refunds to buyers if you have a returnless refund rule enabled in your return settings or if the order value is less than $25 for a non-US return." "A-to-z Guarantee claims: If a buyer submitted an A-to-z Guarantee claim and the investigator determined that you were at fault, we may issue a refund to the buyer and deduct the amount from your Seller Central account." "Chargebacks…" and "Customer Service refunds: If a buyer reaches out to Amazon customer service about a return-related issue, customer service may issue a refund on your behalf to protect the customer's experience." Return shipping: "Customers pay when they change their mind, like accidental orders, with fees deducted from their refund. For issues like defective items or shipping errors, sellers cover the costs." "For seller-issued refunds, you can apply a restocking fee for damaged or materially different items, or returns not covered by the Amazon Return Policy." ("For FBA orders, Amazon handles returns": paraphrase; exact wording not found on the page, 2026-11-10.) [official] (sell.amazon.com blog, "How to manage Amazon customer returns for seller-fulfilled orders", Mickey Toogood, 2025-04-15, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/manage-customer-returns, read 2026-11-09.)

**A5. Seller-fulfilled refund deadline (dated change 2026-01-26).** "Effective January 26, 2026, the Fulfilled by Merchant (FBM) refund process will be updated … The processing window for refunds will extend from two business days to four calendar days before Automated Refund triggers." "If you don't process a refund within four calendar days of receiving a returned item, we may issue an automatic refund. In these cases, you won't be eligible for Seller Assurance for e-Commerce Transactions (SAFE-T) claim reimbursement except for specific situations such as when returned items are lost in transit or delivery confirmation was incorrect through no fault of your own." Amazon names the Guided Refund workflow (GRW), "which allows you to grade returned items, apply appropriate restocking fees, and upload evidence". [official] (Seller Forums News, "Update to seller-fulfilled refund process by January 26, 2026", News_Amazon, posted 2025-12-23 (page `datePublished`), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/11268c92-5a5c-4125-9e59-49e433d0d151, read 2026-11-09.)

**A6. Prepaid return labels (US seller-fulfilled; dated change 2026-02-08).** "Effective February 8, 2026, all US sellers must use the Amazon Prepaid Return Label (APRL) program for customer returns of seller-fulfilled orders regardless of item value. [official] (News_Amazon, "Prepaid return labels required for high-value items", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/9fa92037-c19b-4f03-82b5-7221bd0efa71 and …/c1eae42b-ead4-4c63-a007-aefbca44a867, read 2026-11-09.) Earlier: international sellers without a US return address must "issue a returnless refund or provide a prepaid international return shipping label within two days of a return request", or "we may refund the customer on your behalf and charge the amount to your seller account" (effective 2024-09-16). [official] (News_Amazon, "Update to international returns", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/682481b7-54ca-40e3-b578-7a98988817e3, read 2026-11-09.) The label's cost and who pays it are in the seller-fulfilled returns report (`label_cost`, `label_to_be_paid_by`) and, as money, in Finances `AdjustmentEvent` type `PostageBilling` ("The amount paid by a seller for shipping labels") / `PostageRefund` (§ I).

**A7. FBA returnless and partial refunds (dated).** Launch, ≈2024-08: "We've launched FBA Returnless Resolutions, a new program that helps you streamline returns by offering customers a full refund without having to return the item." ### B. A-to-z Guarantee claims

- "For assigned claims, we will reimburse the buyer and debit your Seller Account or cancel any future payment, as applicable." "If you do not respond to our request for additional information within 72 hours, we will grant the Claim in favor of the customer, and your account will be debited for the Claim amount." "Once a Claim is granted to the customer and your account has been debited, you have 30 calendar days to submit an appeal". [official, via search snippets of Amazon's A-to-z Guarantee claims G27951 and Appeal an A-to-z Guarantee claim G202041210, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G27951, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G202041210; pages are JS shells; snippets read 2026-11-09.]
- Debit and defect are separate: Amazon can debit the claim "but have not counted the claim against your Order Defect Rate" (Amazon notification text quoted by a seller) [opinion, relayed].
- Buy Shipping "Claims Protected" labels shield "Package didn't arrive" claims when conditions are met (quoted by sellers from the help page) [opinion, relayed]. Who funds a protected claim is Amazon's; the seller is not debited [seat, from "protection"; open].
- In Finances v0: `GuaranteeClaimEventList`, same item shape as a refund (A1). In the Payments Transaction view, "A-to-z Guarantee Refund" is a filter (survey `04-job-money.md` §1). Data Kiosk: `claimsAmount` ("Monetary amount of filed A-to-z guarantee claims"), `claimsGranted`, and in seller analytics `accountHealthAtozClaimOrderCount` ("A-to-z Guarantee claim that was not denied, applies to seller-fulfilled orders only"). [official] [command-verified]
- "Orders with an A-to-z Guarantee claim are not eligible for SAFE-T claims." [official, via search snippet of Reimbursement for seller-fulfilled orders G202188810.]

### C. Chargebacks

"Fraud-related: We're responsible for handling payment-related fraud chargebacks, like stolen credit cards or other payment fraud attempts." "Service-related: Amazon sellers are responsible if the chargeback is due to a service-related reason, like non-receipt of the product." Flow: "Amazon notifies the seller involved in a service-related chargeback and requests information related to the order. The seller reviews the Amazon chargeback alert and has the opportunity to issue a refund or represent their case by providing evidence that the transaction was valid. The customer's bank makes the final decision". "the final outcome potentially taking 90 days or longer"; "respond within seven calendar days of the email date". Generic mechanics: "If the bank decides in favor of the seller, the credit is reversed and the funds are returned to the seller." Account Health shows "Chargeback claim" rates for seller-fulfilled and FBA orders separately, "divided by the number of orders in a 60-day period". [official] (sell.amazon.com blog, "How to manage Amazon chargebacks", Mickey Toogood, 2025-12-01, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/chargebacks, read 2026-11-09.) In Finances v0: `ChargebackEventList` (refund item shape, A1); a won representment returns money as a later event [seat; the model names no reversal type for chargebacks]. Data Kiosk seller analytics `accountHealthChargebackClaimOrderCount` ("applies to both seller-fulfilled and amazon-fulfilled orders"). [official] [command-verified]

### E. FBA inventory reimbursements: scope, automation, valuation

**E1. What the policy covers (Amazon staff summary, posted 2026-08-13).** "Warehouse lost", "Warehouse damaged", "Shipment to Amazon (Missing from Inbound)", "Removals lost or damaged", "Customer return not received — … If the customer was refunded but the item was never returned, in most cases we'll charge the customer and reimburse you. If a customer returns your item to a fulfillment center within 60 days of the refund, we'll first determine the condition of the returned item. If the returned item is sellable, we'll return the item to your inventory. If the returned item is unsellable, we'll determine who is responsible for the condition. If Amazon is responsible for an unsellable return, we'll reimburse you and will not add the item back to your inventory. Amazon will not reimburse you for returned items that are damaged by a customer". Ineligible accounts: "Accounts in blocked, terminated, or fraud status aren't eligible". [official, staff] (Indy_Amazon, "FBA Lost Your Inventory? Steps to File a Reimbursement Claim", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/0e07abc1-9026-4540-a005-153d9e78bf04, read 2026-11-09.) The FBA customer returns policy adds: "our reimbursement decisions are based on the actual physical condition of the item when evaluated at our fulfillment center, not the buyer's selected reason." [official] (G200379860 v24, listing seat 2026-10-14.) A seller (pricing in £, posted 2025-04-14 on the .com forum) quotes the customer-return reimbursement policy: "If customer does not return your item to an Amazon fulfillment centre within 45 days of the refund (return less refund), we will issue a reimbursement and credit your account for the item." **J3. Amazon Resale (formerly Amazon Warehouse).**
- Amazon's reimbursement policy text: reimbursed units, "including lost items that are found after reimbursement, may be listed for sale on Amazon warehouse or other channels" (policy PDF, E4a) [official]. - The rename: Amazon announced Amazon Warehouse as Amazon Resale in a corporate blog post in August 2024 ("That's where Amazon Resale comes in", quoted by Digital Commerce 360, 2024-08-20, https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2024/08/20/amazon-resale-replaces-warehouse-with-rebrand/); UK: "Amazon checks, refurbishes, tests and resells returned items" (ChannelX, 2024-10, https://channelx.world/2024/10/amazon-renames-amazon-warehouse-to-amazon-resale/) [opinion, press quoting Amazon; the aboutamazon post itself was not found]. That Amazon Resale "features only Amazon Retail products, and not items from third party sellers" was seen only in search snippets of trade press (EcommerceBytes, unreachable) [snippet].
- **What follows for money** [seat]: a seller's unit sold through Amazon Resale is Amazon's unit by then (reimbursed, or found after reimbursement), so the seller's money on it is the reimbursement (and any reversal, E7), never resale proceeds. A seller-owned used offer of the same ASIN, on the other hand, is the seller's Grade and Resell SKU (J1). The two are told apart by the seller of record, not the condition.

- `F` (+): "missing inventory has been found".
- `N` (+): found, "incorrectly assigned … transferred to your account or because you received an Amazon reimbursement".
- `O` (−): correction, "incorrectly received … or because Amazon has reimbursed you for them".

- `P / Q`: disposition change.
- `3 / 4`: product redefinition.

- Gerpgo computes lost as `M − F − N` (§ Chinese ERPs).

**Seat reading** [seat]. Three vocabularies carry the same events:
- The reimbursements report `reason` is CamelCase with underscores (`Lost_Warehouse`, `Reimbursement_Reversal`).
- The settlement amount-description and v0 AdjustmentType are UPPER_SNAKE (`WAREHOUSE_LOST`, `REVERSAL_REIMBURSEMENT` / `COMPENSATED_CLAWBACK`).
- The ledger uses one-letter reasons (`M`, `E`, `F`).

No published crosswalk exists. Amazon names none, so a join by FNSKU, date and quantity is the practitioners' route.

Seen values: `amzn.gr.TW-BLU-BTH-4-iTlG9GVkiK8EKmva-LN`, `amzn.gr.LUX-PK2-TWL-TEL-qyCObLH5LN9JG-VG`, `amzn.gr.BTH-SHT-WHT-600-u72fnT-Qj6l-r-LN` (a US seller's live data, 2026-08; https://github.com/farhankauser-web/Daily-Sales-Dashboard, `docs/PHASE2_FBA_COMPONENTS_VALIDATION.md` § K1) [cv]; `amzn.gr.A5-BOOK-COVER-DESIGN-5-QaQJXV-PO` (a French store, https://github.com/icingerpower/AmzBooks, `AmzBooksLib/inventory/InventoryMoveTree.cpp`) [cv]. A German DATEV exporter matches `^amzn\.gr\.(.+)-([A-Za-z0-9]{10,20})-([A-Z0-9]{2})$` (https://github.com/donchrillo/jtl2datev, `core/verbringung_pricing.py`) [cv]. The ecomEngine guide (Jeanne Croteau, updated 2026-01-12, https://www.ecomengine.com/blog/fba-grade-resell) says the SKU is "based on the graded condition and type of defect". That `LN` / `VG` stand for Like New / Very Good is [seat]; Amazon publishes no code list.
- The join is fragile: AmzBooks strips the prefix and the last two dash parts to find the seller SKU, and falls back to a manual table for "~20 % of cases where Amazon shortened the SKU" [cv]. jtl2datev tries stems with one to three trailing segments removed, then the ASIN [cv].
- Where they show: a US seller saw amzn.gr MSKUs in Data Kiosk `economics` with `BaseFbaFulfilmentFee` and `FuelSurcharge` components (July 2026: 220 of 3,057 fee charges; about $2,062 of $303,803.40) [cv]. The same project books Date Range transaction rows whose description contains "grade and resell" as other income in one importer and as a logistics cost in another (`unified_txn_importer.py`, `finances_importer.py`) [cv]; the literal row text is not in the repository. A Spanish ledger tool separates "Unidades Grade & Resell (seller SKU "amzn.gr.…" o condición distinta de nuevo)" from sellable stock (https://github.com/jenfol92/AWS_businessIntelligense_Ia, `ledgerCountryStock.ts`) [cv]. A seller asked in Amazon's Ask Amazon thread about other system SKUs, "SKU's named Amazon.Found and some numbers" (78c455ed, 2024-11-21).
- No public settlement file, A2X or Link My Books page, or Chinese ERP help page on amzn.gr SKUs was found (searched 2026-11-10).

**How tools book a graded resale (cost of the unit).**
- sellerboard ("Handling Amazon Grade & Resell SKUs Correctly in Profit Analytics", 2026-01-22, https://blog.sellerboard.com/?p=3309): "COGS is deducted when the original (new) SKU is sold"; "The returned item does not return to sellable inventory, so its cost is not added back"; "If the graded item is sold and COGS is deducted again, the same physical unit is counted twice". It detects Grade & Resell SKUs automatically, shows the original SKU's COGS "for transparency", and "That COGS is excluded from profit calculations when the graded item is sold"; manual COGS on these SKUs "does not affect profit calculations".
- ConnectBooks ("How to Map Grade & Resell Items in Amazon", https://help.connectbooks.com/en/articles/13763093-how-to-map-grade-resell-items-in-amazon): map every Grade & Resell SKU to a non-inventory item "Grade & Resell", or "enter $0 as the Item Cost"; "Amazon fees still apply. Only the product cost is left out."
- jtl2datev (German DATEV export of intra-EU movements): values unmatched graded units ("B-Ware") at 10% of the purchase cost of the matched stem, floor €0.01, or a flat €0.10 [cv].
- Daily-Sales-Dashboard: excludes every `amzn.` MSKU from fee-impact analysis ("a DIFFERENT ECONOMIC EVENT from new-unit fulfilment") [cv].
- [seat] These differ on one point: whether the unit's cost was already written off (refund with an unsellable return, sellerboard and ConnectBooks) or is still carried (a returned unit put back as inventory, then regraded). Which holds depends on how the seller booked the return; it is the seller's choice.

**Fees and recovery, vendors' figures.** Seller Labs (2025-05-21, https://www.sellerlabs.com/blog/fba-grade-and-resell-strategy-2025/) prints a US processing fee table by size tier, charged "Only when items are successfully relisted", "no fee if the item is unsellable"; figures under Facts for human readers only. Synder's glossary: the fee is charged "for each unit that's successfully graded and relisted" and "covers Amazon's cost of inspecting, repackaging, and relisting the item" (https://synder.com/fees-amazon/grade-and-resell-fees/). SPS Commerce (Jacqueline Nance, updated 2026-08-07): "Sellers receive proceeds from the resale, less applicable program fees"; "Exact fee structures should be double-checked in Seller Central". ecomEngine: graded-SKU feedback shows on Voice of the Customer "by looking for the amzn.gr prefix".

**Seller reports.** "My experience with 'Grade and Resell' is that products end up returned as 'Unsellable', even if they are 100% new" (3fa4fa3c); "most of the grade and resell products were returned as defective" (ef44a47a).

**Chinese.** AMZ123 (2025-11-19, https://www.amz123.com/kx/L2cgS471): "亚马逊分级与转售"（Amazon Grade and Resell） has expanded from the US to the UK, Germany, France, Italy, Spain and Japan; sellers get "原价30%-80%的回收款" (30–80% of the original price); "预计可为卖家挽回20%-50%的退货成本" (expected to recover 20–50% of return cost); "部分卖家反馈该服务的定价偏低且流程较为耗时" (some sellers say pricing is low and the process slow). No source is named for the figures. No Lingxing, Gerpgo, Sellfox or ECCANG help page on amzn.gr SKU pairing was found.

## Stable enough for a skill

1. A customer refund is not the mirror of the sale: the charge lines (principal, tax, shipping, gift wrap, promotions, points) are reversed per `ItemChargeAdjustmentList` / `PromotionAdjustmentList`; the referral fee comes back less a refund administration fee; the FBA fulfilment fee is not returned [official model and fee schedule; fulfilment-fee point is practitioner, see Open questions].
2. The refund administration fee is per line item, capped per store in local currency, and a partial refund returns a proportional referral fee (UK relayed help text; US fee schedule) — the cap and rate are read at run time.
3. Refunds, guarantee claims and chargebacks share one item shape in Finances v0 and carry an `OrderAdjustmentItemId`; in v2024-06-19 they carry `ORDER_ID` / `REFUND_ID` related identifiers and an `ORDER_ADJUSTMENT_ITEM_ID` item identifier, so each joins back to its order and order item [official model].
4. Listing transactions by `ORDER_ID` finds refunds, chargebacks and adjustments that post "days, weeks, or even months later" [official guide].
5. Who triggers a refund on a seller-fulfilled order: the seller, refund at first scan (APRL), a seller's returnless rule, Amazon's automated refund after the deadline, A-to-z, chargeback, customer service [official, sell.amazon.com].
6. A-to-z: an assigned claim refunds the buyer and debits the seller or cancels a future payment; an unanswered request for information grants it; a granted claim can be appealed; an order with an A-to-z claim is not SAFE-T-eligible [official, snippets]. The response time and the appeal window are read at run time.
7. Chargebacks: Amazon carries fraud-related ones; the seller carries service-related ones; the bank decides [official]. The reply window and how long the outcome can take are read at run time.
8. SAFE-T is the seller-fulfilled route to recover a refund the seller did not cause; its window (read at run time) and its exclusions are dated facts; an automated refund after the seller's deadline forfeits it except lost-in-transit or wrong delivery confirmation [official].
9. FBA reimbursements cover warehouse lost, warehouse damaged, missing from inbound, removals lost or damaged, and customer returns not returned or returned unsellable through Amazon's fault; customer-damaged returns are not reimbursed [official].
10. Most warehouse-lost/damaged and customer-return cases are reimbursed without a claim; removals always need a manual claim; the IDR portal excludes missing-from-inbound, removals and disposals [official].
11. Value: losses before a customer order are paid at the seller's sourcing cost (Amazon's estimate unless the seller entered one), excluding shipping, handling, duties; losses after a customer order at the order's sale price minus fees; unsellable units at "a reduced estimated cost"; effective 2025-03-31 [official].
12. A reimbursement can be cash or a unit put back; a reversal points to the original by `original-reimbursement-id/-type`; Amazon reverses when the payment was wrong or the unit is found [official columns and relayed help text].
13. The claim windows are dated facts with three Amazon statements that disagree (F); a skill states each with its date and source and does not pick one.
14. Several identifiers chain a reimbursement to its cause: `amazon-order-id` (customer-return cases), `case-id` (manual claims), `fnsku` (inventory), `reimbursement-id` ↔ `original-reimbursement-id` (reversals); the ledger's `Reference ID` names the inventory event [official columns; the chain is seat].
15. Returnless and partial refunds are seller-funded refunds with no unit back and no reimbursement; they count toward the return rate; FBA Returnless Resolutions has no downloadable report; partial refunds show in the FBA Returns dashboard as "Complete – Return not expected" [official].
16. Seller-fulfilled return labels bought through Amazon are charged to the seller when the return reason is the seller's (`PostageBilling` / `PostageRefund` in Finances; `label_cost`, `label_to_be_paid_by` in the returns report) [official].
17. Data Kiosk economics counts refunds "by refund request time" and its `netProceeds` has no reimbursement term; the settlement and Finances count by posting date; seller analytics returns by ship date; the FBA returns report by FC processing date [official schemas and pages].
18. The Finances v0 `AdjustmentType` list in the model has eight values; sellers see more in responses and settlement files; read the value, do not assume the list is closed [official model; practitioners].
19. No API issues a refund, files a claim, answers an A-to-z/chargeback/SAFE-T case, sets returnless or partial-refund rules, or enters sourcing cost [command-verified].
20. A returned unit that is neither restocked nor reimbursed can still bring money back as a Grade and Resell sale. That sale is an ordinary order of the seller's under an Amazon-generated SKU beginning `amzn.gr`, under the same parent ASIN, with referral, fulfilment and (where charged) processing fees [official, J1].
21. An amzn.gr SKU joins back to the seller's own SKU only through its string and the parent ASIN; Amazon gives no linking field [official prefix and parent ASIN; practitioners for the string shape].
22. A donated unit costs the usual disposal fee and brings no proceeds; the yearly donation certificate lists quantities and descriptions, not values [official].
23. A unit sold through Amazon Resale is Amazon's, after a reimbursement; the seller's money on it is the reimbursement [official policy text; the money reading is seat].

## Open questions

1. **Which claim windows Amazon's forms enforce.** The 2025-10-15 News post, the ≈2026-08 staff post and the relayed customer-return policy (45 vs 60 days; 105 days vs 18 months) disagree. A live account shows it in the claim tools' eligibility messages and in when automatic customer-return reimbursements land relative to the refund date.
2. **Automation start date per store.** 2024-11-01 or 2025-01-15 (two Amazon posts with the same text); possibly two store rollouts. Reimbursements report `approval-date` and empty `case-id` per store would show.
3. **FBA fulfilment fee on a refund.** Whether any refund line returns any part of it (e.g. when Amazon is at fault), read from `ItemFeeAdjustmentList` on real refunds.
4. **How the refund administration fee is labelled** on this account: practitioners read settlement `RefundCommission` (and v2024 breakdown `RefundCommission`) as it; whether v0 `FeeType` uses the same name, and whether Data Kiosk economics' fee list carries it (its `PreviewFeeType` enum has `REFERRAL_FEES` and no refund-administration value) [command-verified for the enum].
5. **`listTransactions` by `SETTLEMENT_ID`** — settled from Amazon's documents (verifier, 2026-11-10): not a filter (release note 2026-07-29 announced it; model added 2026-10-28, removed 2026-10-29 by db2936aa; live reference lists `FINANCIAL_EVENT_GROUP_ID` and `ORDER_ID` only). `listSummary` takes it.
6. **`transactionType` values** in v2024-06-19 for refunds, A-to-z, chargebacks, SAFE-T, reimbursements, reversals and reserves on a real account (the model lists only `Shipment`).
7. **Reimbursement `reason` values** beyond those seen in public files (P-ref-35), and the crosswalk between report `reason`, settlement `amount-description` and ledger `Reason` on one account (Amazon publishes none).
8. **How a reimbursement is valued in practice**: compare `amount-per-unit` with the seller's entered sourcing cost (Seller Central only) and with the order's sale price for post-order losses; the "exactly half" report (UK forum) is one seller's case.
9. **Whether reimbursements appear in v0 as `AdjustmentEvent` `FBAInventoryReimbursement` only**, or also under other names, and whether `Quantity`/`FnSKU` are filled.
10. **Buy Shipping-protected A-to-z claims**: whether the debit ever posts and is reversed, or never posts.
11. **Chargeback won**: which event returns the money (type and identifiers).
12. **Replacement-not-returned reimbursement**: under which order id it posts (original or replacement) and after how many days.
13. **Returnless and partial refunds in Finances**: how they are distinguishable from an ordinary refund (description, breakdown or context), since no report lists them.
14. **Seller-fulfilled return label charges**: whether they post as `PostageBilling` adjustments, under which `amount-description`, and joined to which order.
15. **Support API role**: whether the seller's app can be granted Selling Partner Support (Restricted), and what `subject` text reimbursement cases carry.
16. **The Grade and Resell processing fee as a line**: its `amount-type` / `amount-description` in settlement V2, its v0 `FeeType` and its v2024-06-19 `breakdownType`; whether it rides on the graded sale or posts separately at relisting; and whether a US fee is charged today and at what rate (the only figures found are a vendor's).
17. **Graded sales in Finances**: whether a graded sale posts as an ordinary `ShipmentEvent` / `Shipment` with the amzn.gr SKU in `SellerSKU` / `ProductContext.sku`, and whether `getOrderItems` shows `ConditionId` `Used` with the matching `ConditionSubtypeId`.
18. **The amzn.gr SKU on this account**: what the two-character suffix codes are (LN, VG, PO … seen), and how often the seller SKU inside it is shortened so that it no longer matches.
19. **Donations and disposal in the removal report**: which `order-type` and `disposition` values tell a donated unit from a recycled one, and under which line the disposal fee posts (`DisposalComplete` / `FBADisposalFee` are practitioners' names, fulfilment-charges.md).

## Positions

Each position is a fact a skill may state, with its sources (Amazon's first). None is a rule of behaviour. Positions marked [seat] enter no skill.

- **P-ref-1.** A customer refund reverses the sale's charge lines (principal, tax, shipping, gift wrap) and its promotions. The lines are listed per item in `ItemChargeAdjustmentList` and `PromotionAdjustmentList`, and refunds, guarantee claims and chargebacks share one item shape. Source: Finances v0 model `ShipmentItem` [official, command-verified].
- **P-ref-2.** On a refund, Amazon returns the referral fee minus a refund administration fee. The fee is charged per refunded line item and capped per store in that store's currency, and on a partial refund the referral fee comes back in proportion to the price refunded. The rate and cap are read from the store's fee schedule at run time. Sources: Selling on Amazon fee schedule G200336920 v119 (US); UK help text relayed on the Seller Forums (3e43da0c) [official]. Read at run time: Amazon's page Selling on Amazon fee schedule (G200336920), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G200336920, as the seller's own store shows it, and the amount actually retained in a posted refund's fee lines (`listTransactions` breakdowns; v0 `ItemFeeAdjustmentList`).
- **P-ref-3.** Practitioners report that the FBA fulfilment fee does not come back on a customer refund. No Amazon page read says so, and the Finances model neither lists nor excludes such a line, because `FeeType` is an open list. Sources: Seller Forums threads 22c4dcb2, f29481bb [opinion]; financesV0 [command-verified]. A skill can state this only as practitioners' report until a live refund's `ItemFeeAdjustmentList` confirms it.
- **P-ref-4.** On seller-fulfilled orders, refunds come from the seller and also from Amazon on the seller's behalf: refund at first scan (prepaid labels), a returnless rule the seller set, the automated refund after the seller's deadline, A-to-z claims, chargebacks and customer service. A refund in the seller's books is therefore not always one the seller issued. Source: sell.amazon.com "How to manage Amazon customer returns for seller-fulfilled orders" (2025-04-15) [official].
- **P-ref-5.** From 2026-01-26, a seller-fulfilled return that is not refunded within four calendar days of receipt may be refunded automatically. Such an auto-refund cannot be recovered through SAFE-T, except when the item was lost in transit or the delivery confirmation was wrong. Source: News "Update to seller-fulfilled refund process by January 26, 2026" [official]. Read at run time: Amazon's page "Update to seller-fulfilled refund process by January 26, 2026", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/11268c92-5a5c-4125-9e59-49e433d0d151 (a Seller Forums News post on sellercentral.amazon.com, read 2026-11-09); no SP-API operation gives the deadline.
- **P-ref-6.** From 2026-02-08, US seller-fulfilled returns use Amazon Prepaid Return Labels whatever the item's value, apart from the named category exemptions. Labels bought through Amazon post as `PostageBilling` (and `PostageRefund` when a label is refunded) in Finances v0. The returns reports carry `label_cost` and `label_to_be_paid_by`. Sources: News 9fa92037; financesV0 `AdjustmentType`; Returns Reports page (2026-10-28) [official].
- **P-ref-7.** A restocking fee can apply to a seller-issued refund for a damaged or materially different item, or for a return outside Amazon's policy. In Finances it appears as the `RestockingFee` charge type. Return shipping is deducted from the refund when the buyer changed their mind, and paid by the seller when the item was defective or misshipped (`ReturnShipping`). Sources: sell.amazon.com 2025-04-15; financesV0 `ChargeComponent` [official].
- **P-ref-8.** FBA returnless and partial refunds are refunds the seller chose to fund. The unit stays with the buyer and no reimbursement follows. They count toward the return rate, and FBA Returnless Resolutions has no downloadable report. Partial refunds show in the FBA Returns dashboard as "Complete – Return not expected". Returnless refunds do not appear in the FBA customer returns report or the seller-fulfilled returns reports, but Data Kiosk seller analytics `returnedUnits` counts them ("including returnless refunds"). Sources: News ab90097c, d31d6e3f, 8f6ea88b, 5935f007; G3RZC8DVQCDTCQ3B v8; G200453320 (listing seat); Returns Reports page (2026-10-28); `analytics_sellerAnalytics_2025_03_31` [official].
- **P-ref-9.** For an assigned A-to-z claim, Amazon refunds the buyer and debits the seller or cancels a future payment. A request for information left unanswered for 72 hours grants the claim. A granted claim can be appealed within 30 calendar days. An order with an A-to-z claim is not eligible for SAFE-T. Sources: G27951, G202041210, G202188810 via search snippets [official]. Read at run time: Amazon's pages A-to-z Guarantee claims (G27951), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G27951, and Appeal an A-to-z Guarantee claim (G202041210), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/G202041210, as the seller's own store shows them (JavaScript pages, read here only through search snippets on 2026-11-09); no SP-API operation gives the response time or the appeal window.
- **P-ref-10.** Amazon carries fraud-related chargebacks. The seller carries service-related ones, such as non-receipt, and can refund or represent within seven calendar days. The bank decides, and the outcome can take 90 days or more. Source: sell.amazon.com "How to manage Amazon chargebacks" (2025-12-01) [official]. Read at run time: Amazon's page "How to manage Amazon chargebacks", https://sell.amazon.com/blog/chargebacks (sell.amazon.com, US, read 2026-11-09); no SP-API operation gives the reply window.
- **P-ref-11.** SAFE-T reimburses seller-fulfilled refunds the seller did not cause. In Finances v0 it posts as `SAFETReimbursementEvent` (with `SAFETClaimId` and `ReasonCode`) and the `SAFE-TReimbursement` charge. The returns report carries the claim id, state and reimbursement amount. The US window has been 30 days since 2026-02-16, counted from the later of the return delivery scan and the refund date, or from the last scan for a lost shipment. India has excluded packaging damage and broken seals since 2025-08-01. Sources: News d95b0e44, 3fc74ac2; financesV0; Returns Reports page [official]. Read at run time: Amazon's page Reimbursement for seller-fulfilled orders (SAFE-T, G202188810), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/external/G202188810, as the seller's own store shows it (a JavaScript page, unread here), and the News post "US SAFE-T claim filing window changes to 30 days on Feb 16", https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/d95b0e44-60fc-4c16-b690-3626c9a3c43c (US, read 2026-11-09); no SP-API operation gives the window.
- **P-ref-12.** FBA inventory reimbursement covers units lost or damaged in a fulfilment centre, shipments missing from inbound, removals lost or damaged, and customer returns that were refunded but not returned, or returned unsellable through Amazon's fault. Returns damaged by the customer are not reimbursed. Neither are accounts in blocked, terminated or fraud status, nor (US, shipments created from 2026-01-01) units that arrive without proper prep and labelling and are then damaged or untraceable. Sources: Indy_Amazon staff post 0e07abc1; Jameson_Amazon 5c63f045; G200379860 v24 [official].
- **P-ref-13.** Amazon reimburses most warehouse-lost, warehouse-damaged and customer-return cases without a claim, and records them in the Reimbursements report. Every removal claim is still filed manually. The IDR portal leaves out missing-from-inbound, removals and warehouse disposals. The start date is stated twice: 2024-11-01 (News 6605eede, posted 2024-07-24) and 2025-01-15 (News d0fc997e, posted 2024-10-10); no page says which store each addresses. Sources: News 6605eede / d0fc997e, b175b700 [official].
- **P-ref-14.** Since 2025-03-31, a unit lost or damaged before a customer order is reimbursed at the seller's sourcing ("manufacturing") cost: the cost of sourcing or making the item, excluding shipping, handling, customs duties and other costs. Amazon applies its own estimate unless the seller enters a cost on Manage Your Sourcing Cost. A unit lost or damaged after a customer order is reimbursed at the order's sale price minus applicable fees. A unit that was unsellable when lost is reimbursed at "a reduced estimated cost". Sources: News 68864b6f (posted 2024-12-18), ed8abc33 (posted 2025-02-28); staff post 0e07abc1 (2026-08-13) [official].
- **P-ref-15.** The 2025-03-31 date replaced the announced 2025-03-10. The page Amazon first called Manage Your Manufacturing Cost is now Manage Your Sourcing Cost. Source: News ed8abc33 [official].
- **P-ref-16.** A claim window is a dated fact, and Amazon's pages state different windows. Page by page (each re-read 2026-11-10; posting dates from the page):
  - News 6605eede (posted 2024-07-24, effective 2024-10-23), News d0fc997e (posted 2024-10-10, effective 2025-01-09) and EU News 1101821c (posted 2025-01-15): FC lost/damaged "no later than 60 days after the item was reported lost or damaged"; customer returns "between 45-105 days after the customer refund or replacement date"; removal lost in transit "15-75 days from the shipment-creation date"; other removals "within 60 days of the shipment being delivered back to you".
  - News d8f9a56c (posted 2025-08-14, effective 2025-10-15): lost or damaged "Within 60 days of reported loss or damage"; customer returns "Between 45-105 days after customer refund or replacement"; removal lost "Between 15-135 days from shipment creation"; removal damaged "120 days from shipment delivery date"; removal grading "120 days from date item was removed from our fulfilment network"; re-evaluation "Within 90 days of initial claim". It names no missing-from-inbound window.
  - Staff post Indy_Amazon 0e07abc1 (posted 2026-08-13): warehouse lost/damaged "within 60 days of the date the loss or damage event appears in your reports"; missing from inbound "after your shipment is closed/finalized … and within 9 months"; customer returns "no sooner than 60 days after the customer refund … and within 18 months"; removals "within 18 months of the shipment being delivered or lost".
  - gs.amazon.cn articles 250305-3 (2025-03) and 250807-3 (2025-08): missing from inbound within nine months of delivery to the FC (US).
  - Policy text relayed by a seller (b12b6190, 2025-04-14): a unit not returned "within 45 days of the refund" is reimbursed.
  - Valuation dispute: "within 60 days after we have issued the reimbursement" (policy PDF, 2024-12-18).
