## What this covers

Scope: how one product's earnings are assembled from what Amazon reports (sales, refunds, every Amazon charge, ad cost, reimbursements) and what the seller alone knows (cost of goods, freight, duty, prep, packaging, samples, overhead), and the choices inside that assembly: unit of account, how a shared charge is spread, which date a line belongs to, how landed cost is computed, which exchange rate, VAT, variations, bundles, marketplaces, returns, promotions and ad spend. Siblings itemise the fees (what each charge is), refunds, and reconciliation of payouts and settlements; this file uses their results as lines in a product's earnings and does not redefine them. The stock playbook (brief 307) owns storage, aged-stock, low-inventory and capacity charges as stock costs; this file only places them on a product.

**What is the ads pack's, not ours.** `research/beyond/goals-and-economics.md` in the ads playbook (https://github.com/comchatx/cx-sponsored-ads-playbook, read 2026-11-09) owns: break-even ACOS and ROAS and the "margin before ads" they rest on (its sections A–C), TACoS and its identities (J), marginal vs average returns (N), returns as a margin input for ad targets (O), Amazon Points (P), price against advertising (Q), the dated US fee letters 2026 and 2027 as inputs to break-even (C), and its positions P-goals-and-economics-53 and -56 (the Economics dataset's fee types and its net proceeds definition, stated for ad targets). This file cites those and does not restate them. Ours: the product's whole earnings after the fact (and forward with `economicsPreview`), ad cost appearing as a line (the Economics `ads[]` field, the finances "cost of advertising" line), and how a seller spreads ad spend not tied to one ASIN. Ad attribution itself and the Ads API are the ads seat's.

Feeds: the pack's profit skill (working name `product-profit`) and a reference `references/economics-dataset.md` (the Economics fields as Amazon defines them), plus a reference `references/profit-choices.md` (each choice and what it changes, picking none). The reconciliation skill (a sibling's) uses the dating facts here.

Why a senior operator cares: every pricing, ad, reorder and kill-a-SKU decision rests on "what does this SKU make". Amazon now computes its own version (Profit Analytics, SKU Economics, the Economics dataset), the seller's tools compute others, and they disagree for knowable reasons: date basis, refund dating, which charges are on the product row, which cost method, which exchange rate, VAT. Someone who ran this for a large seller knows those reasons by heart; the model does not, and a wrong basis silently misstates every SKU in the same direction.

## Amazon's own rules

### A. The Economics dataset: Amazon's own per-product net proceeds

All from *analytics_economics_2024_03_15.graphql* (local clone `models/schemas/data-kiosk/`, [command-verified] by reading the file; the same file on GitHub, https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models/blob/main/schemas/data-kiosk/analytics_economics_2024_03_15.graphql, last commit 2025-12-22, read 2026-11-09). [official]

- **What it is.** `economics`: "seller economics data for the seller's account aggregated by date and product identifier. The seller economics data consists of aggregated sales, fees, and cost for selected period. The data sets are refreshed daily with the latest sales, fees, and cost data."
- **Net proceeds.** `NetProceeds`: "calculated as sales.netProductSales - sum of fees.charge.totalAmount - sum of ads.charge.totalAmount - (cost of Merchant Fulfilled Network (MFN) products or cost of Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) products) * sales.netUnitsSold." `perUnit` is "calculated by dividing total by sales.netUnitsSold". `netProceeds` "remains unchanged regardless of whether sales, fees, ads, or cost are selected in the query."
- **Sales.** `orderedProductSales`: "calculated by multiplying the price of products and the number of units sold for the selected time period." `refundedProductSales`: "The amount of refunded product sales for the selected time period, it's counted by refund request time." `netProductSales`: "orderedProductSales - refundedProductSales". `unitsOrdered`; `unitsRefunded` ("The number of units refunded in the selected time period"); `netUnitsSold`: "unitsOrdered - unitsRefunded". `averageSellingPrice`: "dividing the orderedProductSales by unitsOrdered … null when unitsOrdered is 0."
- **Fees.** `fees[]` of `FeeSummary` (`feeTypeName`, e.g. "FBA fulfillment fees"; `charges[]` of `Fee`; the single `charge` is `@deprecated` "Use the `charges` field"). Each `Fee` has `aggregatedDetail`, optional `components` (requested with `includeComponentsForFeeTypes`, enum `FeeType`: `FBA_FULFILLMENT_FEE`, `FBA_STORAGE_FEE`), `properties` ("Example: Product Size Tier"), an `identifier`, and its own `startDate`/`endDate`.
- **The arithmetic of a charge.** `AggregatedDetail.totalAmount`: "calculated as amount - promotionAmount + taxAmount". `amount`: "Amount calculated by rate card." `promotionAmount`: "deducted from amount when computing totalAmount." `taxAmount`: "added to amount". `quantity`: "Charged quantity … When the fee is not charged on a per unit basis, this value may be null or may not be an integer." `amountPerUnit`: "calculated by dividing totalAmount by quantity … null when quantity is null."
- **Fee changes split the range.** "when the request date range includes the Amazon fee change date … the response will split the request date range and display the fee separately … Example: If the request date range is [2024-07-01, 2024-07-30] and the Amazon fee change date is 2024-07-15, then the request date range will be split into [2024-07-01, 2024-07-14], [2024-07-15, 2024-07-30]." `amountPerUnitDelta` "is mainly used to indicate the change in amountPerUnit before and after an Amazon fee change … null if there are no Amazon fee change in the selected time range".
- **Ads.** `ads[]` of `AdSummary`: "The advertisements spend data summary for the seller's account aggregated by transaction event type: charge." `adTypeName`: "for example: "Sponsored Products charge"". The supported fee types and "advertisement charge types can be found [here](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/GZ8Y22NL2FSRY8M5)" — the SKU Economics help page (section C), unreachable as text.
- **The seller's costs.** `cost` (`PerUnitCost`): "The off Amazon cost provided by the seller for this product (per unit). The off Amazon cost can be provided on [SKU Central](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/skucentral?mSku=xxx) and [Revenue Calculator](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/revcal)." "Fields in this type will be null if not provided by the seller." Fields:
  - `costOfGoodsSold`: "Cost to manufacture or acquire the product … applicable to both Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) and Merchant Fulfilled Network (MFN) products."
  - `fbaCost.shippingToAmazonCost`: "Cost of shipping the Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) item to an Amazon fulfillment center."
  - `mfnCost.fulfillmentCost`: "Cost of fulfilling an order containing the product, including labor, packing material, shipping to customer, customer service etc."
  - `mfnCost.storageCost`: "Cost to store a single unit of the product per month."
  - `miscellaneousCost`: "Miscellaneous costs not related to this specific product, ex: overhead, etc."
  The schema gives one value per field per product; it says nothing of effective dates or batches.
- **Granularity and dates.** `aggregateBy.productId`: `CHILD_ASIN`, `MSKU`, `PARENT_ASIN` ("By default … MSKU"); `aggregateBy.date`: `DAY` ("aggregated by day against marketplace local time"), `WEEK` ("Sunday is the first day of the week"), `MONTH`, `RANGE` (default DAY). `startDate` "must be no more than two years ago"; a mid-week or mid-month start or end "in UTC+0 timezone" is moved to "the following Sunday" / "the first day of the next month" (start) and "the preceding Saturday" / "the last day of the previous month" (end). `fnsku` "may be null when product aggregation is MSKU and the row is associated with more than one FNSKU"; `msku` may be null under FNSKU aggregation "associated with more than one MSKU" — text left from before `FNSKU` was removed from the enum on 2025-12-22 (SP-API Release Notes, `web01/sp-api-release-notes.md` line 443, [command-verified]). `parentAsin` is always present.
- **Currency.** Every `Amount` has "Currency code of the amount in ISO 4217 format". `marketplaceIds` is a list. The schema states no conversion.
- **Retention.** Every field carries `@resultRetention(duration : "P30D")`; "the shortest (minimum) retention is applied to the query."
- **Forward and simulated.** `economicsPreview`: start "not earlier than today", end "no more than 120 days after today", `feeTypes` from `PreviewFeeType` (referral, closing, per-item, FBA fulfilment base and total, low-inventory-level, inbound placement, monthly storage base and total, storage utilization surcharge, aged inventory surcharge, high return rate, return processing, digital services FBA and SOA, Pan-EU oversize, Sponsored Products charges), MSKU and RANGE only, list at GQNVDV46U9JQ25HY. `economicsSimulation`: `actionTypes` `AWD` or `SIPP` only, "Maximum 100 products per request", end "no more than 90 days after today"; `ProductInputContext` defaults: `historicalAverageWeeklySales` "average weekly sales in the past 90 days", `sendToAWDQuantity` "12 weeks of Historical Weekly Sales", `shipmentLeadTime` "1 week", `unitPerCarton` "20 units per carton", `itemPrice` and `shippingCost` "default value of the listing".
- **New fee types (2026-09-30).** "Updated the Analytics Economics dataset with deal, coupon, removal, disposal, and fuel fees." (SP-API Release Notes, September 30, 2026, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/sp-api-release-notes, saved `web01/sp-api-release-notes.md` line 85, [command-verified].) `feeTypeName` is a string, so new types can appear in `economics` output without a schema change; `PreviewFeeType` in the 2026-10-29 clone has none of them [command-verified].

### L. Import VAT, GST and import consumption tax: who pays at the border, who recovers it (gap fill 2026-11-10)

Customs duty is in sections I and *Facts for human readers only*. This section is the import tax charged on the goods' value at the border, recoverable by a registered importer in each regime read. Rates are human-only.

- **L1. UK, postponed VAT accounting (PVA).** "Accounting for import VAT on your VAT Return means you declare and recover import VAT on the same VAT Return, rather than paying it upfront when the goods are imported and recovering it later. "You do not need any approval". It applies when "the goods you import are for use in your business and you have the right to dispose of them (usually as the owner)" and "you include your VAT registration number on your import declaration". Non-established persons: "you'll need to get someone to deal with customs for you … you must tell the person dealing with customs on your behalf to both: select this on your import declaration [and] enter your details as the consignee." Someone importing on your behalf (freight forwarder, customs agent, broker, express operator) "must have this written confirmation before they can proceed". "You cannot change how you want to account for import VAT on your import declaration once you've submitted it." Consignments of £135 or less follow the online-marketplace rules instead. ### The choices, and what each changes

Each line: the choice, who is documented using which [opinion], and what the choice moves [seat]. None is a recommendation.

1. **Unit of account: per unit, per order, per period.** Amazon's Economics gives per period per product and divides by net units (A); Lingxing's 订单利润 is per order + MSKU; ManageByStats and Lingxing settlement profit are per period. [seat] A per-unit figure from a period total divides charges of one set of units by the count of another (Amazon's staff say so, C); per-order figures leave account-level charges outside the order.
2. **Date basis: order, ship, posted, released/settled, transferred.** Order (My Real Profit, Gerpgo sales view, Lingxing order profit, Sellerboard per A2X), ship (Lingxing option), posted (Nova for fees and refunds), released/settlement (Lingxing default, Dianxiaomi, A2X, ManageByStats), transfer (赛狐 option). [seat] Order basis shows the month's selling but carries estimates until settlement; settlement basis matches cash and statements but shifts sales across months (DD+7, settlement cycles); a month closed early on order basis changes later.
3. **Refunds: against the original sale's period or the refund's.** Economics: refund request time (A). Refund date: Gerpgo, My Real Profit, Nova (posted). Original order: Lingxing setting; A2X says Sellerboard. Settlement that processes it: A2X. [seat] Refund-period dating makes a product's margin in the refund month look worse and the sale month better; original-period dating restates closed months.
4. **Cost method: FIFO, moving weighted average, periodic/month-end average, dated cost periods, fixed value, specific batch.** FIFO (Sellerboard, InventoryLab, Nova, Lingxing, 赛狐), moving WA (Lingxing), average (A2X, Finaloop, Sellerboard), dated periods (Jungle Scout, Sellerboard), fixed (Lingxing, 赛狐), one cost per SKU (Amazon's SKU Central field, A). [seat] When unit costs move (a tariff, a freight spike), FIFO charges old cost until old batches sell out, average blends at once, and a single SKU Central value applies one cost to every unit in the range; recalculating history (Sellerboard, Lingxing) changes past months, forward-only (SellerApp) does not.
5. **Landed cost: what enters, and the spread key.** Freight by volume, actual, volumetric or chargeable weight, carton (ConnectBooks, Lingxing, 卖家之家); duty per SKU on its customs value (ConnectBooks; CBP valuation, I); insurance by value; divisor: the batch's actual units (晨飞). Amazon's own `shippingToAmazonCost` is one per-unit number (A). [seat] Spreading by units over-costs small, light items and under-costs bulky ones; duty on customs value is SKU-specific and does not follow weight.
6. **Shared Amazon charges: on the product row as Amazon charged them, or spread by a key, or left at account level.** Amazon's Economics puts storage, placement and similar fees on the MSKU row (its `FBA_STORAGE_FEE` components, the preview fee types, A); Helium 10 keeps storage and other store-level fees off product rows; keys used: sales share (Lingxing default, Nova for subscription, 易仓), units (Lingxing option, ManageByStats), storage share (易仓), equal split (MRP for SB), by product/brand (ManageByStats), time spread (Sellerboard, 赛狐 daily). [seat] Sales-share spreads favour slow, bulky SKUs (the ones that cause storage); cause-based assignment (by the ASIN that incurred it) needs the charge's own product key, which Amazon gives for storage and placement but not for a subscription.
7. **Storage timing: billing month or month incurred.** Lingxing offers both. [seat] Amazon bills the prior month's storage in the next month; billing-month booking shifts storage one month later than the units it belongs to.
8. **Ads not tied to one ASIN.** SP per advertised SKU (all tools); SB/SD at account level (Helium 10, ConnectBooks), equally across advertised ASINs (MRP, SellerSpace), by sales share (Tool4seller, Eightx fallback), campaign ASINs automatically (Lingxing); report vs invoiced charge gap spread by sales (Lingxing). TACoS-share allocation (each ASIN takes ad spend in proportion to its share of total sales) vs attributed allocation: Eightx, Adspert. Economics `ads[]` gives Amazon's own per-product ad charges (example: SP). [seat] Attributed allocation gives each ASIN the ads that named it, including spend whose sales landed on other SKUs; TACoS-share assumes ads lift every ASIN alike.
9. **Brand Registry, Vine, samples, coupons, deals.** Profit Analytics names deals and coupons among ad costs (B); Economics gains deal and coupon fees from 2026-09-30 (A); Vine enrolment fees and Vine units given away, samples: no Amazon per-product field found; tools treat them as expenses (ManageByStats, 积加 other expenses). [seat] A Vine unit is a unit shipped with no sale: its COGS is a cost with no revenue line.
10. **Reimbursements and liquidation proceeds.** Helium 10's MCP lists reimbursements as a P&L line; Nova dates them by posting; no tool page read assigns them to the product's period of loss. Whether Economics includes reimbursements: not stated (*Open questions* 7). [seat] Booking a reimbursement in the month received lifts that month's product margin for a loss recorded earlier.
11. **Currency: rate at order, at settlement, at payout, period-end, monthly fixed, live.** ACCS converts at the payout's initiation date (H); Sellerboard last day of the period; Lingxing monthly custom rate (delayed orders at booking date); Gerpgo live rate, costs at last recalculation. [seat] A cost paid in CNY and a sale in USD converted at different dates puts FX movement into product margin.
12. **VAT and sales tax: inclusive or exclusive.** Amazon EU prices are VAT-inclusive (H); tools show ex-VAT sales with an inclusive option (Lingxing, 赛狐, Sellerboard "net sales"); Sellerboard can treat fee VAT as recoverable and add VAT to ads; Profit Analytics includes "Tax information (when selected)" (B); Economics adds `taxAmount` into each charge's `totalAmount` (A). [seat] Margin on a VAT-inclusive price understates it by the VAT share for a VAT-registered seller who recovers input VAT; whether Amazon or the seller remits (deemed supplier or not) decides whose liability the VAT is.
13. **Variations and parents.** Economics aggregates by child or parent ASIN (A); Profit Analytics by SKU, ASIN, parent (B). [seat] Parent-level hides a loss-making child; MSKU-level splits one ASIN sold under several SKUs (FBA and FBM, condition variants).
14. **Bundles.** No Amazon field and no tool page read covers bundle cost assembly. [seat] A bundle's COGS is the sum of its components' costs at the cost method in use; component stock consumed by a bundle sale has to come out of each component's batches.
15. **Multi-marketplace.** Economics per marketplace with its currency; Pan-EU/EFN stock may sit outside the store's country (Lingxing); Lingxing falls back across countries for missing freight values. [seat] Landed cost differs by destination (duty regime, freight lane), so one global COGS per SKU misstates each marketplace's margin.
16. **Returns and unsellable units.** Economics nets refunded units from net units and multiplies cost by net units (A); Sellerboard's recovery value and "Assume that pending returns are unsellable"; Gerpgo 退货补回成本; Lingxing 货损一键入账 (survey note). [seat] By Amazon's formula a refunded unit carries no cost at all, whether it came back sellable, came back damaged, or never came back; a seller who loses the unit is under-costed in Economics' net proceeds by that unit's cost.
17. **Promotions and coupons.** Economics' `promotionAmount` reduces a charge, not sales; coupon fees follow "the final price after that coupon has been redeemed" (listing playbook §H). Tools: Lingxing 促销折扣, Gerpgo 促销折扣, MRP "Discounts". [seat] Whether a discount sits as lower revenue or as a cost line changes the margin percentage, not the profit.
18. **Estimates for unsettled orders.** Tools estimate fees for pending orders and replace them (MRP, InventoryLab, Jungle Scout, Lingxing, Sellerboard's PPC dashboard uses "historical averages from the previous month"). [seat] Recent days' profit is a forecast until Amazon posts the charges.
19. **What relieves inventory: settlement units, or the ledger's movements.** Units in each settlement × cost, refunds optional (A2X); ConnectBooks' former financial-event method; the ledger's dated movements (ConnectBooks now, 领星 roll-forward, 优麦云 batches); Amazon's Economics charges cost on net units sold only (A). [seat] Settlement-unit relief leaves lost, damaged, disposed and removed units in the inventory asset until a manual adjustment, so month-end inventory is overstated and COGS understated by their cost; ledger relief books them in the month they happen.
20. **When a lost or damaged unit becomes a cost: at the ledger event, or when a reimbursement arrives.** At the event date, reimbursement booked as a recovery (ConnectBooks blog and help; IAS 2 "in the period the write-down or loss occurs"; CAS 1 第二十一条 「计入当期损益」); at reimbursement (ConnectBooks' former method). [seat] Booking at reimbursement keeps the unit on the books between loss and payment; if the reimbursement is not netted against the written-off unit, the same unit counts once as stock and once as income.
21. **Shipped but not settled.** 领星 names 发出商品 (inventory moved out at shipment, to 主营业务成本 at settlement); A2X relieves at settlement; Economics and order-basis tools at the sale. [seat] On a settlement basis, units shipped in the last days of a month are neither in Amazon's ending warehouse balance nor in that month's COGS unless a shipped-not-settled balance is kept.
22. **Import tax in landed cost: in or out.** Out for a registered importer who recovers it (Zmartly; IAS 2 para 11 *(snippet)*; UK PVA, NL Article 23, JP 仕入税額控除, CA ITC, AU deferred GST, L); in for an unregistered seller, for a seller who is not the importer (DDP/包税 through a forwarder's VAT number; Remote Fulfillment, where the customer is importer), or where recovery is partial. Duty stays in cost in all of these (ConnectBooks, Zmartly, IAS 2 summary). [seat] Loading recoverable import VAT into unit cost overstates every unit's cost by the import tax share; leaving out unrecoverable import tax understates it. PVA and deferral change cash timing, not whether the tax is a cost.
23. **Unsellable, aged and slow stock: carried at cost, written down, or written off when disposed.** IAS 2 and CAS 1 第十五条: lower of cost and net realisable value at the balance-sheet date; ConnectBooks keeps unsellable units in a separate "FBA Unsellable" warehouse; Sellerboard's unsellable-return recovery value (above); 领星 次品 as an inventory state. No vendor page read computes a net-realisable-value write-down from Amazon data. [seat] Carrying unsellable units at full cost until disposal moves the loss into the disposal month.

## Positions

Each is a fact or discipline a skill may state, with its source. None is a rule of behaviour; none picks a method.

**P-prof-1.** Amazon's Data Kiosk Economics dataset defines a product's net proceeds as net product sales minus the sum of Amazon fees charged, minus the sum of ad charges, minus the seller's entered per-unit cost times net units sold; per unit is that total divided by net units sold. Source: *analytics_economics_2024_03_15.graphql* (`NetProceeds`). [official]

**P-prof-2.** In that dataset net product sales are ordered product sales minus refunded product sales, refunds counted by refund request time, and net units sold are units ordered minus units refunded. Source: same schema (`Sales`). [official] (Also P-goals-and-economics-56, ads pack.)

**P-prof-3.** Because the seller's unit cost is multiplied by net units sold, a refunded unit carries no cost in Amazon's net proceeds, whether it came back sellable, damaged or not at all. Source: same schema (`NetProceeds` formula; arithmetic of Amazon's definition). [official]

**P-prof-4.** Each Amazon charge in the dataset is the rate-card amount minus its promotion amount plus its tax amount; its per-unit figure is that total over the charged quantity, which can be null or fractional for charges not made per unit. Source: same schema (`AggregatedDetail`). [official]

**P-prof-5.** When a requested range crosses an Amazon fee-change date, the dataset returns that fee in two date ranges split at the change, with the per-unit change in `amountPerUnitDelta`. Source: same schema (`Fee.startDate`/`endDate`, `amountPerUnitDelta`). [official]

**P-prof-6.** The dataset aggregates by child ASIN, parent ASIN or SKU and by day, week (Sunday start), month or whole range in marketplace local time; week and month ranges snap to whole weeks and months; the start can be at most two years back; FNSKU aggregation was removed on 2025-12-22. Source: same schema; SP-API Release Notes (December 22, 2025). [official]

**P-prof-7.** The dataset's results are kept 30 days (`@resultRetention` P30D on every field). Source: same schema. [official]

**P-prof-8.** The seller's own costs reach the API only as the dataset's `cost` field: cost of goods, FBA shipping to Amazon, MFN fulfilment cost, MFN monthly storage per unit, and miscellaneous cost, each per unit, entered by the seller in SKU Central or the Revenue Calculator, null when not entered. No SP-API operation writes them. Source: same schema (`PerUnitCost`); models operation list (`tools/ops.tsv`, survey note §4.7). [official; command-verified]

**P-prof-9.** The schema gives one value per cost field per product and states no effective date or batch, so whether a cost change rewrites past ranges is not documented. Source: same schema (absence). [official] (Live answer: *Open questions* 2.)

**P-prof-10.** Costs typed into the Revenue Calculator are not saved; it recalculates net profit each time a field changes. Source: Revenue Calculator help GWYBC38TZGCUNRKW *(snippet)*; *The Revenue Calculator* (Christine_Amazon, 2024-11-11). [official] A seller reports COGS reached SKU Economics only once entered in SKU settings (forum 2025-01-03). [opinion]

**P-prof-11.** `economicsPreview` previews fees per SKU for a range starting today and ending at most 120 days ahead; `economicsSimulation` simulates only AWD and Ships in Product Packaging for up to 100 products and 90 days, with stated defaults (past-90-day weekly sales, 12 weeks to AWD, 1-week lead time, 20 units per carton, the listing's price and shipping). Source: same schema. [official]

**P-prof-12.** Amazon's correction of 2024-07-02 says "The Economics dataset requires the Selling Partner Insights role" (and that the Sales and Traffic and Vendor Analytics datasets require Brand Analytics), while Role Mappings and the Data Kiosk API page list "Brand Analytics" as the one required role for every Data Kiosk operation. The Data Kiosk API page says Data Kiosk "determines whether the requester has the required roles for each field in the query upon query creation", so the `createQuery` reply shows which applies to a seller's app. Source: *Correction: Data Kiosk role requirements* (JackE_Amazon, 2024-07-02); *Role Mappings for SP-API Operations* (updatedAt 2026-10-28); *Data Kiosk API* (updatedAt 2026-10-20). [official; re-read 2026-11-10]

**P-prof-13.** Amazon added deal, coupon, removal, disposal and fuel fees to the Economics dataset on 2026-09-30; fee type names arrive as strings, so names not in the schema's enums can appear in results. Source: SP-API Release Notes (September 30, 2026); schema (`feeTypeName: String!`). [official]

**P-prof-14.** Amazon's own profit view in Seller Central is Profit Analytics (Reports → Selling Economics and Fees): ASIN-, SKU- and parent-level profitability over the past two years and next 90 days, with off-Amazon costs, price simulation, and (from November 2026) a Fee Change Comparison dashboard; it counts selling, fulfilment, storage, return processing, removal and disposal fees, Sponsored Products, deals and coupons, and tax when selected. Amazon announced it on 2025-09-17 as "introduced … this year" and reachable then; the News post naming the Reports path is dated 2025-11-13. No SP-API operation serves the dashboard. Source: *Amazon announces new tools to help sellers save time and money* (aboutamazon, 2025-09-17); Accelerate forum post (2025-09-17); *Track and optimize your unit economics with Profit Analytics* (2025-11-13); Yujiro_Amazon (forum, ≈2026-01); 2027 US fee letter (2026-11-05); models operation list. [official; command-verified]

**P-prof-15.** SKU Economics (SKU Central, 2024) shows the same net proceeds; its per-unit fee divides fees charged in the period by net units sold in the period, and Amazon notes the units charged a fee in a period can differ from the net units sold in it: an item sold in one month and shipped in the next is "charged FBA fees" in the next, and the SKU Economics card "may not include taxes on some fees, while other reports do". Source: SKU Economics announcement (2024-04-15); Topher_Amazon and Manny_Amazon (forum, 2026-05-14). [official]

**P-prof-16.** `getOrderMetrics` gives units, order items, orders, ordered product sales and average unit price per interval for one marketplace, filterable by ASIN or SKU (not both), fulfilment network, buyer type and Amazon program; it carries no fees, refunds or costs. Role: Product Listing (NA, EU). Source: `sales.json`; *Role Mappings for SP-API Operations*. [official; command-verified]
