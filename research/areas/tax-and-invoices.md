# Tax and invoices at a glance, for a money reader

Read 2026-11-09 by the research seat. Tags: [official] Amazon's or a tax authority's own page or model; [opinion] a named practitioner or vendor; [research] a published study; [seat] this seat's own reasoning, never a position; [command-verified] a command this seat ran over the local models or pages. This is not tax advice. It records what Amazon collects, withholds, reports and invoices, and where each document is.

## What this covers

**Scope.** The tax lines inside a seller's Amazon money, read so that none is mistaken for revenue, cost or fee:
- tax charged to buyers and kept by the seller;
- tax charged to buyers and withheld by Amazon (marketplace facilitator / deemed supplier);
- tax Amazon deducts from the seller (India TCS/TDS, Mexico IVA/ISR);
- tax on Amazon's own fees, and the invoices for those fees;
- the reports and documents that carry tax (sales-tax report, VAT reports, 1099-K, DAC7, Brazil NF-e), with the role each needs;
- (gap fill 2026-11-10) the US state taxes that marketplace-facilitator collection does not touch (physical presence from FBA inventory; WA B&O, OH CAT, TX franchise), and which Amazon report shows where inventory sat (§E);
- (gap fill 2026-11-10) the China side for China-based sellers: platform tax-information reporting under State Council Decree 810, CRS and overseas income, export customs modes and the export VAT refund or exemption routes, and the evidence Amazon's records give (§F);
- (gap fill 2026-11-10) the seller's own inbound: importer of record, import VAT and duty, and Pan-EU transfers of own goods (§G).

It does not cover filing returns, registration strategy or tax planning.

**Skills it feeds.** It feeds every skill that reads a settlement, transaction or Date Range line. Whatever the money pack's earnings and payout skills turn out to be named, they need one reference page, "tax lines and where tax documents live", so that cx never counts a withheld tax line as a cost, a collected tax line as sales, or VAT on fees twice.

**Why a senior operator cares.**
- The most common money misreading is gross "sales" that include tax Amazon then withholds.
- The 1099-K figure is gross of tax and refunds, and it never matches the books.
- In the EU and UK, VAT on Amazon's fees changed from reverse charge to local VAT on 2024-08-01, which changed what a fee "costs".
- A seller whose VAT number is missing in a storage country gets no VAT calculated by Amazon.

## Amazon's own rules

### A. US: marketplace facilitator sales tax
- **A1. Coverage.** Amazon's page "Marketplace Tax Collection" (Amazon.com Customer Service help, node 202211260, https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202211260, read in full by the verifier 2026-11-10) says: "As the marketplace facilitator, Amazon calculates, collects, remits, and refunds sales tax on third party sellers transactions destined to states where this legislation is enacted." It is a buyer-facing help page, not Seller Central. (The original read was through `digprjsurvey.amazon.com`, which is not a documentation host; see Verification.)
  - It lists 48 jurisdictions with effective dates: every state with a statewide sales tax, plus Alaska (2020-04-01, only local jurisdictions that adopted remote-seller rules), DC (2019-04-01) and Puerto Rico (2020-07-01).
  - The earliest are WA (2018-01-01) and PA (2018-04-01). The latest are FL and KS (2021-07-01) and MO (2023-01-01).
  - DE, MT, NH and OR are not listed. They have no statewide sales tax; that reading is [seat].
- **A2. Named exceptions** on the same page:
  - Colorado home-rule cities that have not adopted the ordinance;
  - Alaska localities that did not enact collection;
  - Alabama rentals and leases;
  - New York, where collection "applies only to tangible personal property".
  - Coverage: "This legislation also extends to particular regulatory fees in certain states; when applicable, Amazon calculates, collects, and remits these amounts as well."
  - "In certain states, the tax rate applied to your order may depend on where your package ships from, not just where it's being delivered."
  - All quotes re-read on www.amazon.com, 2026-11-10: 48 rows, WA 1/1/2018 and PA 4/1/2018 earliest, MO 1/1/2023 latest [command-verified].
- **A3. Seller side.** "States with active tax collection laws will be greyed out in your tax settings". The order shows tax collection model "Marketplace Facilitator" and responsible party "Amazon Services, Inc." (News_Amazon, Seller Forums, "Marketplace tax collection overview in the US", c. 2019, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/forums/t/marketplace-tax-collection-overview-in-the-us/488339) [official]
- **A4. The model's words** (financesV0.json, `TaxWithheldComponent.TaxCollectionModel`):
  - "`MarketplaceFacilitator`: Tax is withheld and remitted to the taxing authority by Amazon on behalf of the seller."
  - "`Standard`: Tax is paid to the seller and not remitted to the taxing authority by Amazon."
  - Charge types include `Tax` ("the tax on the principal that is collected by the seller") and `MarketplaceFacilitatorTax-Principal` / `-Shipping` / `-Giftwrap` / `-Other` ("the tax that is withheld on …").
  - The model's own example JSON uses `"TaxCollectionModel": "Free"`, a value its description does not list.
  - [official, command-verified]
- **A5. Retail delivery fees.** Amazon's page "Retail Delivery Fee" (Amazon.com Customer Service help, node GHY2HYVQF8MKXJH4, https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GHY2HYVQF8MKXJH4, read in full by the verifier 2026-11-10) says: "Amazon collects and remits the fee on orders with items sold by Amazon.com or third-party sellers and when the order is delivered to a state that has adopted a Retail Delivery Fee." Also: "The retail delivery fee is non-refundable regardless if a return or replacement of the product occurs." The fee "applies to the entire order regardless of the number of items".
  - Colorado: the page's table reads "Colorado July 1, 2026 $0.31 Per Checkout". The CO DOR figure of $0.28 for July 2025–June 2026 is the original seat's read and was not re-checked.
  - Minnesota: "July 1, 2024 $0.50 $100 of qualifying items".
  - How it shows to the seller is not stated. Finances carries `MPFRegulatoryFee` / `MarketplaceFacilitatorRegulatoryFee-Principal` (04-job-money.md §2.2), and the Date Range columns `regulatory-fee` / `tax-on-regulatory-fee` (A7). [official]
- **A6. Tax Calculation Services (TCS).** Amazon's page "Before you set up tax calculation services" (201706700, snippet only) says it is "available to Professional Sellers who have an obligation to calculate Taxes"; the seller stays responsible for "calculation, remittance, and reporting".
  - Product Tax Codes (G200794510, snippet): "Tax Calculation Services require both Product Tax Code (PTC) and jurisdiction settings to be configured"; the default PTC is set under Settings > Tax Settings.
  - Amazon's fee for TCS (2.9% of tax collected, per Taxomate) was **not found on any reachable Amazon page**.
  - [official, snippet]
- **A7. Where facilitator tax appears in a money line.** The Deferred Transaction Report (`GET_DATE_RANGE_FINANCIAL_HOLDS_DATA`, Payment Reports page, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-payment.md, updatedAt 2026-09-09) has the transaction-view column set:
  - `tax-collection-model`, `product-sales`, `product-sales-tax`, `shipping-credits-tax`, `gift-wrap-credits-tax`;
  - `regulatory-fee`, `tax-on-regulatory-fee`, `promotional-rebate-tax`, `marketplace-withheld-tax`.
  - Tax charged to the buyer sits in the `*-tax` columns. Where Amazon is the facilitator, the same amount comes back out in `marketplace-withheld-tax`.
  - [official]
- **A8. Form 1099-K.**
  - IRS, "Form 1099-K FAQs: General information" (https://www.irs.gov/newsroom/form-1099-k-faqs-general-information, last updated 2026-04-03, full):
    - "The One, Big, Beautiful Bill retroactively reinstated the reporting threshold."
    - TPSOs report when payments "exceed $20,000" and "more than 200 transactions".
    - A TPSO "may still send a Form 1099-K" below that.
    - The current fact sheet is FS-2025-08 (2025-10-23). IR-2025-107 (2025-10-23) says the "dollar limit reverts to $20,000".
    - [official]
  - The superseded path, for reading old notes: FS-2023-27 planned "$5,000 for 2024". Notice 2024-85 (IRB 2024-51, 2024-12-16) set more than $5,000 for 2024, more than $2,500 for 2025 and more than $600 from 2026. P.L. 119-21 (2025-07-04) replaced it. [official]
  - IR-2026-03 (2026-01-08): proposed regulations under which TPSOs need not backup-withhold unless both $20,000 and 200 are exceeded. [official]
  - Amazon, "IRS Form 1099-K FAQs" (https://pay.amazon.com/help/200831230, undated, full):
    - "Form 1099-K is issued ONLY to U.S. persons."
    - It is provided "on or before January 31 of the following year", under Reports > Tax Document Library, to the primary user only.
    - "Unadjusted gross sales mean the total sales, unadjusted for fees or refunds"; shipping is included.
    - [official]
- **A9. The US sales tax report** (`GET_FLAT_FILE_SALES_TAX_DATA`, Tax Reports page, updatedAt 2026-11-03):
  - "Tab-delimited flat file for tax-enabled US sellers. Content updated daily."
  - "This report cannot be requested or scheduled. The seller must generate the report from the Tax Document Library in Seller Central. After the report has been generated, you can search for it using the `getReports` operation."
  - Columns include `Tax_Collection_Model`, `Tax_Collection_Responsible_Party`, `Total_Tax`, `Total_Tax_Collected_By_Amazon`, `Tax_Amount_Collected_By_Amazon`, `Financial_Component`, `Jurisdiction_Level`, `Jurisdiction_Name`, `Taxed_Jurisdiction_Tax_Rate`, `Taxable_Amount`, `NonTaxable_Amount` and `Buyer_Exemption_*`.
  - [official]

### B. EU and UK: VAT
- **B1. VCS.** "Amazon's VAT Calculation Service (VCS) provides sellers with VAT calculation services and invoicing functionality."
  - It is available only in IE, ES, UK, FR, BE, NL, DE, IT, SE and PL, and "exclusively covers VAT data".
  - Three options: Amazon creates invoices; the seller uses Amazon's tax data to make and upload invoices (needs the `invoice upload` feed); or the seller uses its own tax data.
  - "VCS sellers … all prices must remain VAT inclusive in the price feed. VAT exclusive prices are automatically derived."
  - Enrolment is in Seller Central (`/tax/registrations?context=enrollment`) and takes VAT numbers, a ship-from address, a default PTC, an invoicing option and an activation date.
  - Source: VAT Calculation Service guide, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/vat-calculation-service-guide.md, updatedAt 2026-09-09. [official]
- **B2. "VAT not calculated".** "Amazon cannot calculate VAT for [shipments] that are missing a VAT number for the country from which the item was shipped. For these shipments, sellers must perform their own VAT calculation and upload the invoices … `UPLOAD_VAT_INVOICE` … Amazon does not perform any validation on the TotalAmount and TotalVATAmount fields." (same page) [official]
- **B3. Upload deadline.** Self-invoicing sellers upload "within one business day after shipment" (a "24 hour SLA"). Late sellers may lose the badge or be removed from VCS. "Only upload PDF invoices." Source: VCS Best Practices, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/vat-calculation-service-best-practices (helper, full). [official]
- **B4. Invoice corrections.** Customers can only change billing name or address. Amazon then creates a `CorrectionReversal:` REFUND and a `Correction::` SHIPMENT in the VIDR, with unchanged amounts. "Always send partial refunds VAT-exclusive … Amazon uses the VAT rate that was applied for the original shipment." Source: Invoice Compliance and Correction, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/invoice-compliance-and-correction.md. [official]
- **B5. Deemed supplier (EU from 2021-07-01).** The EC OSS portal (https://vat-one-stop-shop.ec.europa.eu/one-stop-shop_en, helper full) says the electronic interface is deemed supplier:
  - for supplies of goods inside the EU "if the underlying supplier is established outside the EU";
  - for imports "in a consignment of a value not exceeding EUR 150" (IOSS).
  - Amazon's Chinese notice (https://globalselling.amazon.com/news/news-notices-210523, 2021-05-23) states the same for China-registered sellers: "配送至欧盟的B2C订单亚马逊将代扣代缴VAT" (Amazon collects VAT on B2C orders shipped to the EU), and "哪里有库存，哪里注册税号" (register a VAT number wherever stock is held).
  - The Orders API v2026-01-01 field `taxCalculationBreakdowns[].reportingScheme` = `UOSS` / `IOSS` marks the scheme "when Amazon acts as the deemed reseller".
  - [official]
- **B6. UK (from 2021-01-01).** GOV.UK: the marketplace is "liable for the VAT" on consignments of £135 or less, and "on goods of any value that are located in the UK at the point of sale" when sold by overseas sellers. Amazon, as relayed by ChannelX on 2020-12-09: "You will not receive the UK VAT amount in your disbursements." [official; Amazon wording via ChannelX]
- **B7. Pan-EU storage.** "Für jedes Land, in dem Waren gelagert werden, ist eine Umsatzsteuer-Identifikationsnummer erforderlich" (a VAT number is needed for every country where goods are stored). "Seit dem 1. Januar 2021 ist das Vereinigte Königreich nicht mehr Teil des Pan-europäischen FBA" (the UK has not been part of Pan-European FBA since 2021-01-01). Source: sell.amazon.de, "Versand durch Amazon in Europa", helper full. [official]
- **B8. VAT Services on Amazon ended.** "we've decided to end the VAT Services on Amazon programme on October 31, 2024". Documents were downloadable "until August 31, 2025". No successor programme was found. Source: News_Amazon, https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/84cd893d-012b-45e4-8502-98c0ea5d1a97. [official]
  - Old settlements carry its fees as Service Fee lines `VatAmazonTechnologyFee`, `VatFiscalRepFee` and `VatPartnerFee` (Lingxing formula page, §5). [opinion]
- **B9. VAT on Amazon's fees.** "Effective August 1, 2024", services "currently supplied by Amazon Services Europe S.à r.l. (ASE) will be supplied by Amazon EU S.à r.l. (AEU)".
  - Sellers established in UK, DE, FR, IT, ES, NL, PL, BE or SE are invoiced by the AEU branch in their country, with local VAT.
  - Everyone else is invoiced by "the AEU head office in Luxembourg", with unchanged VAT treatment.
  - Source: News_Amazon, "Update to Amazon UK and EU agreements and VAT treatment", https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/1264a996-1dee-4b47-8ff7-822f60f6992e. [official]
- **B10. DAC7.** Sellers must give TIN, company registration number and permanent establishments within 60 days of notice and re-declare every 36 months, or Amazon "is obliged to deactivate your account and withhold your funds". Source: News_Amazon, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/fdf56854-c40a-45d5-92a0-335aa0583757. [official]
  - UK platform reporting rules "apply from 1 January 2024, with reporting due from January 2025". Platforms must "provide a copy of the information to the taxpayer" (GOV.UK, "Reporting rules for digital platforms"). [official]
  - Where the seller's DAC7 copy sits in Seller Central, and its date, were **not found**.
- **B11. VCS change on 2026-06-01.** "the VAT Calculation Service will calculate tax at the shipment level by applicable tax rate instead of by individual unit". Source: VAT Calculation Service Updates, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/vat-calculation-service-updates.md. [official]
- **B12. EU customs, low-value parcels.**
  - €3 interim flat duty per item "in small parcels valued at less than €150", from 2026-07-01 to 2028-07-01, abolishing "the threshold-based customs duty relief". Source: Council release of 2026-02-11, reproduced by EAC-NY; consilium.europa.eu returned 403. [official, secondary copy]
  - UK: "This measure will remove the £135 duty relief" (GOV.UK, "Reforming customs rules for low value imports", updated 2026-10-28), with no date given. [official]

### C. Japan
- **C1. Qualified invoices (from 2023-10-01).** In Settings > Tax Settings the registration number field takes "仅允许输入T+13位数字" (only T plus 13 digits). "只有当卖家于亚马逊卖家平台上传JCT注册号后，亚马逊才能代表卖家向买家开具合规发票。" (Only after the seller uploads its JCT registration number to Seller Central can Amazon issue qualified invoices to buyers on the seller's behalf.) Source: gs.amazon.cn, 2023-09-27, https://gs.amazon.cn/news/news-notices-230927. [official]
- **C2. Platform taxation (from 2025-04-01).** 「プラットフォーム課税の対象は、国外事業者がデジタルプラットフォームを介して行う消費者向け電気通信利用役務の提供…」 (platform taxation covers consumer-facing electronic services that foreign businesses provide through a digital platform). It covers digital services, not goods. Source: NTA Tax Answer No.6568, https://www.nta.go.jp/taxes/shiraberu/taxanswer/shohi/6568.htm. [official]
- **C3. Goods from 2028-04-01.** The 令和8年度 reform outline (https://www.mof.go.jp/tax_policy/tax_reform/outline/fy2026/08taikou_04.htm) treats a foreign business's sales of goods in Japan, and low-value (≤ ¥10,000 ex-tax) mail-order sales from abroad, as made by a designated 第２種プラットフォーム事業者 (Type 2 platform operator).
  - The designation procedure applies from 「令和９年４月１日」 (2027-04-01). The first test treats the reform as in force from 2027-01-01 and multiplies the Jan–Mar 2027 sales by 4 against 「50億円（税込み）」. 「上記の指定の効力は、令和10年４月１日に生ずることとする」 (the designation takes effect 2028-04-01).
  - Import consumption tax is waived for goods of registered 特定少額資産販売事業者 (registered sellers of specified low-value goods; the original's 特定少額販売事業者 was a typo). 「令和９年10月１日からその申請を受け付ける」 (applications accepted from 2027-10-01).
  - Enacted: the NTA's page (above) states the revision "was made" (見直しが行われました) and publishes a July 2026 leaflet and Q&A.
  - [official, MOF outline and NTA, re-read 2026-11-10]
  - Whether Amazon.co.jp will be designated: no Amazon statement found. Today Amazon does **not** collect JCT on foreign sellers' goods [seat inference from C2/C3].

### D. Other marketplaces
- **D1. Australia and Singapore.** Amazon's page "Tax on Low Value Imported Goods (LVIG)" (Amazon.com Customer Service help, node TgfSC5JDtZP0P3cmqU, https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=TgfSC5JDtZP0P3cmqU, read in full by the verifier 2026-11-10) says: "Tax is calculated, collected, and remitted by the relevant Amazon entity at the applicable rate in accordance with local legislation in each country."
  - **Scope:** the page covers "items sold on Amazon.com, delivered to addresses in specified jurisdictions … outside of the United States". It describes Amazon.com cross-border orders, not sales on Amazon.com.au or Amazon.sg. AU and SG marketplace collection rests on the IRAS and ATO lines below.
  - The page's table: AU GST 10% (AUD 1,000), SG GST 9% (SGD 400), and also Chile, EU (€150), Jersey, Kazakhstan, Malaysia, New Zealand, Norway, Switzerland and UK (£135).
  - IRAS (updated 2026-09-18): low-value goods are those "not exceeding the GST import relief threshold of S$400" delivered by air or post, and the marketplace "must charge and account for GST", since 2023-01-01.
  - ATO: since 2018-07-01 (page returned 403; snippet only).
  - [official]

### E. US: state taxes that facilitator collection does not touch (gap fill 2026-11-10)
- **E1. What facilitator collection covers.** Amazon's words (A1, A2) cover sales tax and "particular regulatory fees". Nothing on Amazon's page reaches state income, franchise or gross-receipts taxes. [official; scope reading is seat]
- **E2. Inventory in a state is physical presence (Washington).** The WA Department of Revenue page "Marketplace sellers" (https://dor.wa.gov/taxes-rates/retail-sales-tax/marketplace-fairness-leveling-playing-field/marketplace-sellers, read 2026-11-10, no date shown) says:
  - Physical presence includes "Keeping a stock of inventory in Washington (including inventory held by a marketplace facilitator or another third-party representative)."
  - "Marketplace sellers with physical presence nexus, must register to report B&O tax and collect and report sales tax starting on the date the physical presence begins in Washington."
  - "if you have physical presence, you do not need to meet the $100,000 threshold to have nexus in Washington."
  - "Marketplace sellers who meet Washington nexus criteria are required to report and pay B&O tax, even if sales of products or services are only made through marketplace facilitator(s)."
  - "A marketplace seller is not required to collect Washington sales tax on sales made through a marketplace facilitator if the marketplace seller has obtained documentation to prove the facilitator is collecting and reporting the sales tax on their behalf."
  - [official]
- **E3. Texas franchise tax.** It is "a privilege tax imposed on each taxable entity formed or organized in Texas or doing business in Texas" (Comptroller, Franchise Tax page, https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/franchise/, read 2026-11-10).
  - The Marketplace Providers and Sellers page (https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/sales/marketplace-providers-sellers.php, read 2026-11-10) says "A foreign taxable entity (i.e., out-of-state entity) with a Texas use tax permit is presumed to have nexus in Texas and is subject to Texas franchise tax." It also says nexus begins on the earliest of the date of physical presence, the date of a use-tax permit, or the date of $500,000 Texas gross receipts.
  - Rule 3.586 was amended effective 2026-01-07 (dated changes).
  - Each entity with nexus files a report (No Tax Due, EZ or Long Form) plus an information report.
  - The threshold and rates are under Facts for human readers only.
  - [official]
- **E4. Ohio Commercial Activity Tax (CAT).** It is a tax on Ohio taxable gross receipts, not on income. Nexus is a "bright-line presence" test, met by any one of: Ohio receipts at or above a dollar threshold, Ohio property, Ohio payroll, 25% of total property, payroll or receipts, or Ohio domicile.
  - Since 2024 tax is owed only above an annual exclusion. That exclusion rose for 2025 and later (dated changes).
  - [official, Ohio Department of Taxation pages via search snippets; tax.ohio.gov returned 403/404 to every direct read]
  - Whether Ohio counts FBA inventory as "property" for the bright-line test was not found on an Ohio page.
- **E5. California: FBA inventory and franchise/income tax.** The Office of Tax Appeals held two out-of-state FBA sellers liable for California's minimum franchise tax, and in one case minimum income tax, for 2019, on FBA participation and inventory stored in California. The cases are OTA 230613542 (Diet Standards LLC) and OTA 230212546 (Fishbone Apparel Inc.).
  - In the second case the FTB learned of the inventory from the CDTFA (Avalara, "California marketplace inventory creates franchise tax nexus", 2026-03-23).
  - Elliott Davis (2026-04-27 update) says P.L. 86-272 protection "may" be exceeded by inventory-based nexus. It also says states differ, so "there may never be a consensus".
  - A 2022 Pennsylvania court ruling went the other way for sales-tax collection based only on Amazon-stored inventory (search snippet citing an MTC Tax Notes piece; not read).
  - [opinion; OTA opinions not opened]
- **E6. Where inventory sat, from Amazon.** `GET_LEDGER_DETAIL_VIEW_DATA` carries `Fulfillment Center` and `Country` on every movement row, with "all historical movements of your inventory for 18 months". `GET_LEDGER_SUMMARY_VIEW_DATA` takes `aggregateByLocation` = `FC` (stock-playbook research, `areas/stock-states.md` F2–F3).
  - Role: **Amazon Fulfillment** (Role Mappings for Types) [official, command-verified].
  - Amazon publishes no map from FC code to state on any page read. TaxJar ("How to find where Amazon FBA gives you sales tax nexus", 2026-05-07) points sellers to the Inventory Ledger and its fulfillment-center column. It says FC codes are "designated by the closest airport" ("DFW" → Texas, "CHA" → Tennessee). [opinion]

### F. China: the tax face of China-based sellers (gap fill 2026-11-10)
- **F1. Decree 810 (2025-06-20).** 「互联网平台企业应当于季度终了的次月内…向其主管税务机关报送平台内经营者和从业人员的身份信息以及上季度收入信息」 (platforms report, within the month after each quarter, in-platform operators' identity and prior-quarter income) (Art. 4). Other provisions:
  - Platforms verify the data, and are not liable where a seller's own fault made it wrong (Art. 6).
  - On an inspection, tax authorities may demand 「合同订单、交易明细、资金账户、物流等涉税信息」 (orders, transaction details, fund accounts, logistics) (Art. 7).
  - Penalties on platforms run from ¥20,000 to ¥500,000 (Art. 10).
  - 「平台内经营者和从业人员在本规定施行前的涉税信息，互联网平台企业不需要报送」 (information from before the rules took effect is not reported) (Art. 12).
  - 「境外互联网平台企业在中华人民共和国境内提供营利性服务的，按照国务院税务主管部门的规定报送」 (overseas platforms providing for-profit services in China report under the STA's rules) (Art. 12).
  - Source: 国务院令第810号, https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100010/c5241238/content.html, read 2026-11-10. The Q&A by the Ministry of Justice and the STA (2025-06-20) says the first report falls in October 2025. [official]
- **F2. STA Announcement 2025 No. 15 (2025-06-26): who reports for an overseas platform.**
  - 「境外互联网平台企业在境内设立运营主体的，由依法取得增值电信业务经营许可证的境内企业报送」 (if it has a domestic operating entity, the entity holding the value-added telecom licence reports).
  - Failing that, the domestic entity that provides 「商家入驻、店铺运营、营销推广等服务」 (seller onboarding, store operation, marketing) reports.
  - 「在境内未设立运营主体的，由境外互联网平台企业指定境内代理人报送」 (with no domestic entity, a designated domestic agent reports).
  - An overseas platform is one that serves 「平台内境内的经营者和从业人员，或者境内的购买方」 (domestic sellers or domestic buyers).
  - https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5241477/content.html, read 2026-11-10. [official]
  - AMZ123 (卖家之家, 2025-07-01) names 亚马逊卓越有限公司 (licence holder) or 亚马逊（上海）国际贸易有限公司 as Amazon's likely reporting entity. This is speculation. [opinion]
- **F3. What is reported (STA 15).**
  - Identity: name, USCC or taxpayer number, address, store name and unique store ID, contact. For unregistered individuals also ID type and number and country.
  - Income: 「收入总额、退款金额、收入净额」 (gross income, refunds, net income), income from other activity, and 「交易（订单）数量」 (order count).
  - 「收入总额是指…销售款项（全部价款和增值税税额的合计）…不扣除取得平台企业、政府机关、支付机构等实际补贴金额以及向平台企业支付的佣金、服务费等其他费用」 (gross income is all consideration including VAT, not reduced by subsidies or by commissions and service fees paid to the platform).
  - 「收入的确认时间为收讫销售款项或者取得销售款项索取凭据的当日」 (income is recognised on the day payment is received or the right to collect it arises).
  - Foreign currency is converted at the PBOC central parity of the first day of the reporting month or of the transaction day, 「确定后12个月内不得变更」 (unchanged for 12 months once chosen).
  - Errors are corrected 「自发现之日起30日内」 (within 30 days of discovery).
  - Exemptions: 「境外互联网平台内境外的经营者和从业人员的身份信息，境外互联网平台企业无需报送」 (overseas platforms do not report the identity of their overseas-resident sellers). For overseas sellers' sales of *services and intangibles* to domestic buyers, a buyer whose net purchases in the quarter are 5,000 yuan or less may be left out. That exemption does not concern goods.
  - [official]
- **F4. Amazon's report, as Amazon describes it (relayed).**
  - Coverage: "the policy applies to all China-based sellers who sell goods, services, or intangible items to customers across Amazon's worldwide stores … Even if a seller only sells products on Amazon's US marketplace, their account information will still be reported" (China Daily, 2025-10-16, reporting Amazon's notice). [opinion relaying official]
  - The seller's copy comes by email from `donotreply@amazon.com`, titled "View your China tax report for July-September 2025" or 「税务报表」, with a link valid 7 days (AMZ123 跨境电商财税专家, 2025-12-23).
  - Its fields, as sellers downloaded them: 卖家记号 (merchant token), store, company name, USCC, quarter, 收入总额, 退款金额, 收入净额, 「支付给平台的佣金、服务费合计金额」 (total commissions and service fees), and order count.
  - Amazon's FAQ (relayed by the same piece and by AMZ123 一八供应链, 2026-01-17) says the report "预计会有所差异" (is expected to differ) from the Payments and Amazon VAT Transactions reports, because:
    - FX: each order is converted at the PBOC central parity on the day it ships. On weekends and holidays the next working day's rate is used (一八供应链's gloss).
    - Period: "我们会根据订单发货日期每季度报告交易" (transactions are reported by quarter on order ship date).
    - Income: it "包括商品销售收入、运费、礼品包装费、折扣、税费和附加费" (includes item sales, shipping, gift wrap, discounts, taxes and surcharges). Overseas VAT and US sales tax are therefore inside reported income.
    - Fees: it "包括但不限于销售佣金、配送费用、跨境配送运费、监管广告费用和服务订阅月费" (includes, but is not limited to, referral fees, fulfilment fees, cross-border delivery freight, "监管广告费用" [sic; 一八供应链 renders it 赞助广告费用, sponsored ads] and the monthly subscription). 一八供应链 adds storage and inbound placement, and says DSP display/video ads are excluded.
    - [opinion relaying official; the FAQ itself not reached]
  - No SP-API operation, report type or model mentions a China tax report: 0 hits for "china tax" across the models clone, `ops.tsv` and the saved pages [command-verified].
- **F5. Corrections by Amazon.** In early September 2026 Amazon emailed some sellers that it had re-checked "订单、退款、平台费用及币种折算" (orders, refunds, platform fees and currency conversion) in the Q4 2025 report and re-filed corrected data. Some sellers' fee totals rose. One seller's Q1 report showed fees of about 3.6% of sales. Sellers who disputed figures were told the tax bureau "只认平台报送的原始数据" (accepts only the platform's data) (AMZ123 敏哥跨境记 and 跨境电商跨境屋, both 2026-09-04). [opinion]
- **F6. Overseas income and CRS.** Since 2025, provincial tax bureaus (Shanghai 2025-03-26, Zhejiang 2025-03-26, Hubei, Shandong) have published cases of residents who did not declare overseas income. Shanghai: 「居民个人从中国境外取得所得的，应当在取得所得的次年3月1日至6月30日内申报纳税」 (a resident individual declares overseas income between March 1 and June 30 of the following year).
  - STA via Xinhua (2026-01-16): authorities have reminded taxpayers to self-check 2022–2024 overseas income. "因纳税人不进行纳税申报或计算错误造成不缴或少缴税款的，税务机关在三年内可以追征税款、滞纳金" (tax unpaid through non-filing or miscalculation can be recovered with late fees within three years).
  - The STA expert piece (2025-07-14) says China exchanges financial-account information under CRS with "100多个国家和地区" (over 100 jurisdictions) and matches it against individual income-tax filings.
  - Enterprise income tax law, Art. 2 and 3: an enterprise 「依照外国(地区)法律成立但实际管理机构在中国境内」 (incorporated abroad but effectively managed in China) is a resident enterprise taxed on 「来源于中国境内、境外的所得」 (income from inside and outside China).
  - [official: shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn; chinatax.gov.cn; heilongjiang.chinatax.gov.cn; hainan.chinatax.gov.cn copy of the EIT law]
  - Marketplace Pulse ("Chinese Sellers Are Abandoning Mainland Registration", undated, after 2025-11) counts "Over 780 Chinese sellers changed their Amazon legal entity to Hong Kong in November 2025 alone". It says Hong Kong registration "may not provide the protection these relocations suggest" because of place-of-effective-management rules and CRS. [opinion]
- **F7. Export customs modes.**
  - GACC Announcement 2020 No. 75 (in force 2020-07-01) created 「9710」 「跨境电子商务企业对企业直接出口」 (cross-border e-commerce B2B direct export) and 「9810」 「跨境电子商务出口海外仓」 (cross-border e-commerce export to overseas warehouse). The latter is for goods sent to an overseas warehouse and sold through a platform from there (mofcom.gov.cn copy). [official]
  - The other export modes are 1210 (bonded zone), 9610 (cross-border e-commerce retail export) and 0110 (general trade) (乌海市商务局 comparison, 2023-05-24) [official, local government]. 1039 is market procurement.
  - 优品跨境物流 (AMZ123, 2025-11-19) on each [opinion]:
    - 9610 above 5,000 yuan per shipment converts to general trade;
    - 9810 suits FBA;
    - 1039 「免征增值税…0.4 %-1 % 的核定征收」 (VAT-exempt, deemed collection at 0.4–1%);
    - 0110 「退税率最高」 (highest refund rate).
- **F8. 9810 refund on departure (STA Announcement 2025 No. 3, 2025-01-27, in force on publication).**
  - 「在货物报关离境后，即可预先申报办理出口退（免）税…后续再根据货物销售情况进行税款核算」 (after customs departure, the refund may be claimed in advance and reconciled later against sales). The claim is flagged 「HWC-YT」 (overseas-warehouse pre-refund).
  - The reconciliation period runs from the month after the refund is processed 「至次年4月30日」 (to April 30 of the next year). Without reconciliation, the pre-refund is clawed back. Goods still unsold at reconciliation are repaid in full and re-claimed once sold.
  - Filing evidence: where there is no export contract, 「海外仓订仓单、自营海外仓所有权文件、租赁海外仓租赁协议」 (an overseas-warehouse booking note, ownership papers or lease) may be filed.
  - The seller keeps 「销售记账凭证、销售明细账等可以佐证货物已实现销售的资料」 (sales vouchers and sales ledgers proving the goods were sold) within 15 days of the sale.
  - https://fgk.chinatax.gov.cn/zcfgk/c100012/c5238152/content.html; the STA's interpretation (gz.gov.cn copy) has worked examples. [official]
  - 宝通达 (AMZ123, 2025-11-24) says customs dropped "海外仓备案" (overseas-warehouse filing) from end-2024, and names "FBA订仓单" (the FBA booking) as warehouse evidence. [opinion]
- **F9. Zone exemption without input invoices, and deemed-profit income tax.**
  - 财税〔2018〕103号 (from 2018-10-01) gives VAT and consumption-tax exemption to cross-border e-commerce exporters in an approved comprehensive pilot zone (综试区) for 「出口未取得有效进货凭证的货物」 (exports without valid purchase invoices). The conditions are registration in the zone with export details, and declaration through zone customs.
  - STA Announcement 2019 No. 36 (from 2020-01-01) lets such firms pay enterprise income tax on a deemed taxable-income rate applied to 「收入总额」 (total revenue). They must 「准确核算收入总额」 (account revenue accurately).
  - Sources: gov.cn copies, https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-10/17/content_5441297.htm and https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/19/content_5462399.htm. [official]
- **F10. Amazon's fees in a Chinese ledger.** Amazon's fee entities are outside China and issue no Chinese 发票 (VAT invoice) [seat, from B9 and P-tax-12: the fee invoices are Amazon EU, Amazon Services and similar]. STA Announcement 2018 No. 28, Art. 11: 「企业从境外购进货物或者劳务发生的支出，以对方开具的发票或者具有发票性质的收款凭证、相关税费缴纳凭证作为税前扣除凭证」 (spending on goods or services bought from abroad is deducted on the other party's invoice or receipt with invoice character).
  - Where the authority doubts a foreign document, it "可以要求其提供境外公证机构或者注册会计师的确认证明" (may ask for confirmation by a foreign notary or CPA) (Invoice Measures Art. 33, as quoted by Zhejiang tax bureau Q&A, 2026-01-07).
  - [official: gov.cn 2018 gazette; zhejiang.chinatax.gov.cn]
  - 金方圆财务 (§5 of sellers-tools): 「平台扣费必须单独入账，不可直接冲减收入」 (platform fees are booked separately, not netted against revenue). [opinion]

### G. The seller's own inbound: importer of record, import VAT, own-goods transfers (gap fill 2026-11-10)
- **G1. Amazon is not the importer of record.** Dominic_Amazon, "Importing 101 for FBA Sellers: Part 1" (Seller Forums, 2024-07-03, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/dd6a1e0e-d2dd-4592-b6f1-0ecda906a7b1, read 2026-11-10): "Amazon will not act as the IOR for your FBA inventory, regardless of the shipment size or value. You, the seller, or a freight forwarder you work with must be listed as the IOR on all customs documentation". The IOR files the entry and pays "all import duties, taxes, and fees". [official, Amazon staff post]
  - Help page "Import and export inventory" (G201468520, JS shell, snippet only) says Amazon "will not serve as the importer of record for any shipment of FBA inventory". Amazon may appear as ultimate consignee only with "in care of FBA" before its name. [official, snippet]
  - With Amazon Global Logistics: 「要通过亚马逊全球物流（AGL）发货至进口国，您的账户需要有对应进口国的、通过审核的海关登记进口商（IOR）」 (to ship through AGL, the account needs an approved IOR for the import country). The seller signs a POA letting AGL clear customs. Duty is not in the AGL quote. Source: Amazon Seller University CN guide 「跨境物流…清关」, https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/28/AS/AGS/SU/CN_GS_CB_Logistics_Basics_Customs_Clearance.pdf, PDF dated 2026-06-24, read 2026-11-10. [official]
- **G2. UK postponed VAT accounting.** "Accounting for import VAT on your VAT Return means you declare and recover import VAT on the same VAT Return, rather than paying it upfront when the goods are imported and recovering it later." "The normal rules about what VAT you can reclaim as input tax apply." "Your business must be registered for VAT in the UK". The importer tells its customs agent in writing, and checks the "monthly postponed import VAT statement" and C79 on the Customs Declaration Service. Source: GOV.UK, "Check when you can account for import VAT on your VAT Return" (updated 2025-06-16), read 2026-11-10. [official]
- **G3. EU import VAT timing.** The EC VAT Committee working paper (WP 924, centralised clearance) says Article 211 leaves payment rules to Member States, and that "all Member States apply either postponed accounting, or deferred payment or both" [official, search snippet; not opened]. France made postponed accounting mandatory for French-VAT-registered importers from 2022-01-01 (SimplyVAT, Avalara, VATupdate 2025-11-14). Holdwise (NL) names the Dutch Article 23 licence. [opinion]
- **G4. Pan-EU and EFN moves are transfers of own goods.** Under the VAT Directive, moving own goods to another Member State is treated as an exempt intra-Community supply in the departure state and an intra-Community acquisition in the arrival state (Art. 17). The call-off-stock relief (Art. 17a) needs a known buyer and so does not fit marketplace stock [official: EC 2020 quick-fixes explanatory notes; COM(2017) 569, via search; seat on the marketplace fit].
  - Amazon: a VAT number is needed in every storage country (B7). The AVTR lists "cross border inbound, and cross border fulfillment center transfers" (Tax Reports page), with departure and arrival country and the seller's VAT number in each.
  - Practitioners name the rows `FC_TRANSFER` and `INBOUND` (Taxually; Eurofiscalis, §What practitioners add).
  - ViDA introduces a "transfer of own goods" scheme under SVR (EC ViDA page; SVR elements from 2028-07-01). [official]
- **G5. Where the moves show on today's roles.** On **Amazon Fulfillment**, the ledger detail `WhseTransfers` rows carry `Fulfillment Center` and `Country`. The stock playbook notes that Seller Central's "Cross-Border Inventory Movement report" has no documented SP-API type (`stock-playbook/research/beyond/programs-and-regions.md` R3). **The brief's premise, corrected.** The Finances Invoices API 2026-06-25 does **not** return Amazon's VAT invoices for selling or FBA fees to sellers. Its overview says "Availability: Vendors only … The Selling Partner API for Invoices is available for vendors". Its model has `partnerType` SELLER among the enum values and `serviceChargeType` `FBA_INVENTORY_STORAGE_FEE`, but the operations and docs are vendor-facing. No SP-API operation found returns the seller fee invoices that Amazon issues. [official, command-verified]

**Fields Amazon publishes without a closed list.**
- The Finances 2024-06-19 `breakdownType` is open.
- The v0 `TaxCollectionModel` example says `"Free"`.
- The settlement `amount-description` tax names (`MarketplaceFacilitatorVAT-Principal`, `TaxWithheld` on Liquidations, `TaxAdjustment`, `NetworkComminglingTransaction/TaxAmount`, `ReturnPostageBilling_VAT`) come from practitioners' mappings, not an Amazon dictionary.

## Stable enough for a skill

1. A tax line in a settlement, transaction or Date Range row is one of:
   - tax the seller collected and owes (`Tax`, `ShippingTax`, `GiftwrapTax`, `*-tax` columns, `TaxCollectionModel` `Standard`);
   - tax Amazon collected and withheld (`MarketplaceFacilitatorTax-*`, `MarketplaceFacilitatorVAT-*`, `marketplace-withheld-tax`, model `MarketplaceFacilitator`), which nets to zero against the buyer-side tax;
   - tax Amazon deducted from the seller (IN TCS-*, 194-O TDS, MX IVA/ISR, `TaxWithholdingEvent`);
   - tax on Amazon's own fee (`taxAmount` in the economics dataset; VAT on EU/UK fees).
2. Which party remits is stated per item: Orders `taxCollections[].model` and `responsibleParty`; AVTR and VCS report `tax_collection_responsibility`; sales-tax report `Tax_Collection_Model` and `Tax_Collection_Responsible_Party`.
3. The US 1099-K is "unadjusted gross sales", including tax and shipping and not reduced by fees or refunds; it does not equal net sales.
4. The tax documents live in Seller Central's Tax Document Library: 1099-K, US sales-tax reports, Mexican tax invoices, and seller fee invoices (seller fee invoices per forum snippets only).
5. The US sales-tax report reaches the API only after the seller generates it in the Tax Document Library.
6. Every EU/UK VAT and invoice report, the US sales-tax report, India's MTRs, Brazil invoices and buyer tax registrations need **Tax Invoicing or Tax Remittance (Restricted)**, neither of which sellers tick today. The restricted ones also need an RDT.
7. The two tax faces on today's roles:
   - Finance and Accounting: Finances v0 tax and withholding lists, the Date Range/Deferred report columns, Flat File V2 settlement lines, economics `taxAmount`.
   - Amazon Fulfillment (being added): `GET_GST_STR_ADHOC` (IN) and the `TAX_INVOICE_*` notifications.
8. No SP-API operation returns the invoices Amazon issues to sellers for its fees. The 2026-06-25 Invoices API is vendors only.
9. Amazon calculates no VAT for a shipment from a country where the seller's VAT number is missing; the seller must calculate it.
10. Since 2024-08-01, EU/UK fees are supplied by Amazon EU S.à r.l. Which establishments pay local VAT on fees is read at run time (below).
11. VAT Services on Amazon ended 2024-10-31. Its fee lines in older settlements are a closed history.
12. The AVTR returns all stores whatever `marketplaceId` is sent; its window must end some days before the request (read at run time, below); re-pulling a range can return changed rows.
13. In Brazil the invoice is the seller's NF-e; Amazon's API reads FBA invoices and accepts FBA Onsite ones.
14. (gap fill) Amazon's facilitator collection covers sales tax and named regulatory fees. A state may treat FBA inventory held there as the seller's physical presence, and tie registration, gross-receipts (WA B&O, OH CAT), franchise and income-tax obligations to it. Amazon does not collect any of these.
15. (gap fill) Where stock sat is read from the Inventory Ledger (`Fulfillment Center`, `Country`; Amazon Fulfillment), over a history span read at run time (below). Amazon publishes no FC-to-state map.
16. (gap fill) China-based sellers' identity and quarterly income are reported to China's tax authority by every platform, Amazon included, from Q3 2025. Reported income is gross of VAT and of platform fees, and fees are reported as their own total.
17. (gap fill) Amazon's China figure reaches the seller by email, not by any API. Its dating, conversion and income bases are under What cx must read at run time.
18. (gap fill) 9810 export refunds can be claimed on departure and are reconciled against sales by April 30 of the next year. The sales evidence is the seller's sales vouchers and ledgers.
19. (gap fill) Amazon is not the importer of record for FBA inventory; the seller or its agent is, even when AGL clears customs under the seller's POA.
20. (gap fill) A cross-border Pan-EU or EFN move of own goods is a VAT event in both countries. The AVTR records it with departure and arrival country.

## Facts for human readers only

Dated figures. A skill never states these.
- **US 1099-K threshold** (IRS, 2026-04-03): more than $20,000 and more than 200 transactions, restored retroactively by P.L. 119-21 (2025-07-04). Superseded plans: $5,000 (2024), $2,500 (2025), $600 (2026+).
- **US TCS fee:** 2.9% of tax collected (Taxomate 2024; not confirmed by Amazon).
- **US retail delivery fees:**
  - Colorado: $0.31 per checkout from 2026-07-01 ($0.28, July 2025–June 2026).
  - Minnesota: $0.50 on orders of $100 or more, from 2024-07-01.
- **India:** TCS 0.5% (from 2024-07-10; Amazon systems 2024-07-13); 194-O TDS 0.1% (from 2024-10-01).
- **Mexico from 2026-01-01** (KPMG):
  - ISR 2.5% for those with an RFC;
  - IVA withheld 50% (with an RFC) or 100% (without);
  - ISR 20% without an RFC.
- **AU GST** 10% on low-value imports up to AUD 1,000. **SG GST** 9% on low-value imports up to SGD 400 (both from Amazon.com's LVIG page, which covers Amazon.com orders delivered abroad; read 2026-11-10). **UAE VAT** 5%; **KSA VAT** 15%.
- **EU:** €3 per item interim customs duty on parcels under €150 (2026-07-01 to 2028-07-01).
- **Former VAT Services on Amazon pricing** (2022 rate card): €350 per year (€75 partner + €275 technology).
- **Washington** (DOR, read 2026-11-10): remote marketplace sellers without physical presence register for B&O and sales tax above $100,000 combined Washington gross receipts in the current or prior year. Physical presence needs no threshold.
- **Texas franchise tax** (Comptroller, read 2026-11-10): report years 2026–2027 no-tax-due threshold $2,650,000. Rate 0.375% (retail or wholesale) and 0.75% (other). EZ computation 0.331% up to $20 million total revenue. Economic nexus at $500,000 Texas gross receipts.
- **Ohio CAT** (Ohio DOT, search snippets only): rate 0.26% of taxable gross receipts above the exclusion. Exclusion $3 million for 2024 and $6 million for 2025 onward. Bright-line receipts test $500,000; property or payroll $50,000; or 25% of totals.
- **California** minimum franchise tax / LLC tax: $800 (Avalara on the OTA cases, 2026-03-23).
- **China platform reporting:** penalties on platforms from ¥20,000 to ¥100,000, or ¥100,000 to ¥500,000 where serious (Decree 810 Art. 10). The overseas-seller exemption is 5,000 yuan net per domestic buyer per quarter, for services and intangibles only (STA 15).
- **China zone deemed-profit rate:** 4% taxable-income rate on total revenue (STA 2019 No. 36). Practitioners quote 1039 market procurement at "0.4 %-1 %" deemed collection (优品跨境物流, 2025-11-19).
- **One seller's China report** (AMZ123, 2026-09-04): Q1 US sales about 2.5 million yuan, net about 2.3 million, reported fees "9万出头" (just over 90,000 yuan), about 3.6%.
- **Hong Kong** profits tax 8.25% on the first HKD 2 million, 16.5% above (Marketplace Pulse).

## Positions

- **P-tax-1.** Every tax amount in a money line is one of four kinds: buyer tax the seller keeps and owes, buyer tax Amazon withholds and remits, tax Amazon deducts from the seller, or tax on Amazon's own fee. Each is named by its own line. — financesV0.json `ChargeComponent`, `TaxWithheldComponent`, `TaxWithholdingEvent`, TCS-*; Data Kiosk economics `taxAmount` [official, command-verified]; Lingxing 销售税/市场税 split [opinion].
- **P-tax-2.** `MarketplaceFacilitatorTax-*` (US) and `MarketplaceFacilitatorVAT-*` (EU/UK) withheld lines offset the buyer-side tax on the same item, so facilitator tax is neither the seller's revenue nor its cost. — financesV0.json ("Tax is withheld and remitted … by Amazon on behalf of the seller") [official]; A2X ("results in a total of 0") [opinion]; Amazon via ChannelX ("You will not receive the UK VAT amount in your disbursements") [official].
- **P-tax-3.** Tax with collection model `Standard` is "paid to the seller and not remitted … by Amazon": it sits in the seller's payout and remains the seller's to remit. — financesV0.json [official].
- **P-tax-4.** Who remits is stated per item in Orders v2026-01-01 `taxCollections[]` (`model`, `responsibleParty`), the sales-tax report's `Tax_Collection_Model` / `Tax_Collection_Responsible_Party`, and the VCS/AVTR `tax_collection_responsibility`. Amazon tells sellers not to report VAT where it reads MARKETPLACE. — Get tax information; Tax Reports [official]; ChannelX relaying Amazon [opinion].
- **P-tax-5.** The Date Range transaction view and the Deferred Transaction Report carry the tax columns `product-sales-tax`, `shipping-credits-tax`, `gift-wrap-credits-tax`, `tax-on-regulatory-fee`, `promotional-rebate-tax`, `marketplace-withheld-tax` and `tax-collection-model`. "Product sales" excludes them. — Payment Reports [official].
- **P-tax-6.** Amazon collects US sales tax as marketplace facilitator in every US jurisdiction with a statewide sales tax, plus DC and Puerto Rico, with named exceptions: Colorado home-rule cities, Alaska non-adopting localities, Alabama rentals, New York non-tangible goods. The current list is read from Amazon's page, never stored. — Marketplace Tax Collection, Amazon.com Customer Service help node 202211260 (www.amazon.com, re-read 2026-11-10) [official]; eComEngine's stale list [opinion]. Read at run time: Amazon's page "Marketplace Tax Collection" (https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=202211260) for the list, and per item Orders v2026-01-01 `getOrder` / `searchOrders` with `includedData=TAX` (`taxCollections[]`).
- **P-tax-7.** The US 1099-K reports "unadjusted gross sales", which include tax and shipping and are not reduced by fees or refunds. It is issued to US persons only, by January 31, in the Tax Document Library. — Amazon Payments 1099-K FAQ [official]; A2X [opinion].
- **P-tax-8.** The 1099-K threshold changed by law within 2024–2025. Notice 2024-85's $5,000 / $2,500 / $600 path was replaced retroactively by P.L. 119-21 (2025-07-04), restoring over $20,000 and over 200 transactions.
