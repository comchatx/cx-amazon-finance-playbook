# Reconciling a settlement or a month: tying Amazon's lines to the bank deposit and to the books

Read 2026-11-09 by the research seat. Tags: **[official]** Amazon's own page or model (title, URL, date read); **[opinion]** a named practitioner, vendor or developer; **[research]** published study; **[seat]** this seat's reasoning, never a position; **[command-verified]** a command run over a clone of Amazon's models repository (https://github.com/amzn/selling-partner-api-models, commit db2936aa, 2026-10-29) or over pages fetched today.

## What this covers

**Scope.** The documents a seller uses to prove that Amazon's money ties out. These are the settlement report (V2 flat file), Seller Central's payment views and date-range reports, the deferred transaction report, and the Finances, Transfers and summary APIs used as reconciliation sources. It covers how each one is structured, how they key to one another (settlement-id, financial event group, trace ID, order-id), and where they disagree: time zone, posting versus release, the deferral, reserves, negative balances, currency and month boundaries. It also covers the accounting practices sellers and their tools apply on top: clearing account, gross versus net, pass-through tax, reserve as an asset, and the deferral accrual. Fees, refunds, money flow and per-product profit belong to sibling seats. This file states what those lines look like inside the reconciliation documents and goes no further.

**Feeds.** The pack's payouts/reconciliation skill and its `references/` (settlement columns, identifier map, which document counts what and when). It also feeds the accounting-export facts any profit skill relies on.

**Why a senior operator cares.** Every bookkeeper's month-end close is this job. The settlement total is the only Amazon figure that equals a bank line. Every other Amazon figure (Business Reports, the economics dataset, Date Range reports) counts a different set of transactions, or counts them on a different date or clock. Someone who ran this for a large seller knows these points:
- the settlement file is keyed to payout timing, not to the month;
- a settlement that crosses month-end has to be split;
- the reserve is an asset;
- since late 2024 the deferral has moved sales between months;
- and since 2026 the Date Range reports changed meaning.

## Amazon's own rules

### A. The settlement report (Flat File V2) is generated by Amazon, found, not requested

- "Settlement reports cannot be requested or scheduled. They are automatically scheduled by Amazon. You can search for these reports using the `getReports` operation." [official] Settlement Reports, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/report-type-values-settlement.md (updatedAt 2026-10-14), read 2026-11-09.
- V2 entry: "**reportType value**: `GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_FLAT_FILE_V2` · **Role**: Finance and Accounting · **Availability**: Seller Central sellers · **Report output type**: Tab-delimited flat file. A settlement report. This report replaces the Flat File Settlement Report and XML Settlement Report. Price columns are condensed into three general-purpose columns: `amount-type`, `amount-description`, `amount`." [official] same page.
- "The `amount-type` and `amount-description` columns contain the type of charge or fee you have incurred, and indicates whether different fees are related to a common type. Amount values always populate in a single `amount` column … even if new fees or transaction types are introduced in the future. Additionally, amounts are displayed in local currency formats (for example, `95,00` for EU instead of `95.00`)." [official] same page.
- Attribute list, in Amazon's order: `settlement-id`, `settlement-start-date`, `settlement-end-date`, `deposit-date`, `total-amount`, `currency`, `transaction-type`, `order-id`, `merchant-order-id`, `adjustment-id`, `shipment-id`, `marketplace-name`, `amount-type`, `amount-description`, `amount`, `fulfillment-id`, `posted-date`, `posted-date-time`, `order-item-code`, `merchant-order-item-id`, `merchant-adjustment-item-id`, `sku`, `quantity-purchased`, `promotion-id` (24 columns) [official] same page. Amazon gives **no prose definition per column**. The page lists names only.
- The deprecation notice adds format facts: "Provides quantities for all rows (in line with amounts) and posted dates are provided in two, easily-readable formats, `posted-date` (MM/DD/YY) and `posted-date-time` (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC)". "Ensures that you don't have empty cells in the `amount-type`, `amount-description`, or `amount` columns". "Categorizes amounts in the `amount-type` and `amount-description` columns in the same row". The V1→V2 map: "price type name, fee type name (Example: item-related-fee-type) → amount-type"; "price type value, fee type value (Example: Commission) → amount-description"; "price amount, fee amount → amount"; "transaction-type → transaction-type or amount-description"; "price type, fee type (Example: other-fee-reason-description) → amount-description (disjointed amounts may be consolidated)". "There is no XML report replacement." "This change applies to all marketplaces." [official] Deprecation of two payment settlement report types, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/changelog/update-deprecation-of-two-payment-settlement-report-types.md, read 2026-11-09. The dates are struck through on the page: "~~March 25, 2026~~ **November 11, 2026**".
- Removal: "`GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_FLAT_FILE`, `GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_XML` | March 17, 2025 | November 11, 2026". "**Removed** indicates that calls to the resources fail as of the removal date." [official] SP-API Deprecation Schedule, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/sp-api-deprecations.md (updatedAt 2026-09-09), read 2026-11-09 (live page identical to `web01/sp-api-deprecations.md`).
- Retention through the API: `getReports` `createdSince` "The default is 90 days ago. Reports are retained for a maximum of 90 days." [official] reports_2021-06-30.json [command-verified]. A settlement older than about 90 days is not findable through `getReports` [seat, from that sentence; practitioners confirm, §What practitioners add].
- Amazon's staff names three primary payment reports and how they cross-reference: "**Settlement Report**: … including the 'Settlement Period' that covers each payout. **Date Range Report**: … a detailed or summary view of your transactions within a custom date range. **Deferred Transactions Report**: … any payments that have been deferred, including the reason and expected release date. … if you're looking for a specific order payment, you can first check the Transactions view to find the posted date, then use that date to locate the payment in either the Settlement or Date Range report." [official, Amazon staff] Where's My Money? — Mastering Payment Reporting and Reconciliation (Danny_Amazon, 2025-08-26), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/718bf5b9-5500-4e85-942b-7a0a0357354e, read 2026-11-09.
- What is **not** on Amazon's page today: the survey's search-snippet lines "(usually every 14 days) … tied to the disbursement cycle" and "a manual disbursement starts a new report" do not appear on the live settlement page (updatedAt 2026-10-14) [command-verified: full page read]. The 14-day cycle is stated elsewhere by Amazon (§E).

### B. Settlement-level rows: reserve, previous reserve, and what has no order

- Amazon's model names the reserve event: `ReserveEvent`: "A reserve event that is generated at the time a settlement period closes. This occurs when some money from a seller's account is held back." [official] financesV0.json `AdjustmentType` [command-verified].
- The settlement-file labels "Current Reserve Amount" ("The reserve amount is the amount held back in a settlement period to cover potential expenses such as returns and refunds") and "Previous Reserve Amount Balance" ("The reserve amount from the previous settlement report") come from Amazon's field-definition text as reproduced by Intentwise and by wimoor (Chinese/English). Amazon's Seller Central help page is a JS shell to plain GET, so these are cited second-hand [opinion: Intentwise V1→V2 guide; wimoor 亚马逊财务项中文解释].
- `DebtRecoveryEvent`: "A debt payment or debt adjustment", with `DebtRecoveryType` `DebtPayment` / `DebtPaymentFailure` / `DebtAdjustment`, `RecoveryAmount`, `OverPaymentCredit`, `ChargeInstrumentList` (Description, Tail, "The amount charged to this charge instrument"), and per item `OriginalAmount`, `GroupBeginDate` and `GroupEndDate` ("The start date and time of the financial event group that contains the debt") [official] financesV0.json [command-verified]. A negative settlement charged to the seller's card therefore shows in v0 as a debt event tied to the group where the debt arose [seat].
- `FinancialEventGroup.OriginalTotal`: "For a closed financial group, this is the total amount of a disbursement **or a charge amount**. For an open financial event group, this is the current balance." [official] financesV0.json. A closed group can be a charge (money from the seller), not only a payout.

- **Deferred transactions on the dashboard (2024).** "we typically reserve your sales proceeds until seven days after an order is delivered … By November 1, your delivery date reserve amounts will display as Deferred transactions in the Total balance box … Until then, they may still display under the account-level reserve field". The 2021 US page post adds that settlement reports include only "transactions which have been disbursed" (survey, https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/fa4dc3e343d8205930d783e0cf035427, 2021-09-20).
- **Statement view / Transaction view** (survey's reads, reused): the Statement view lines are Beginning Balance, Sales, Refunds, Expenses and Account Level Reserves, with the next disbursement. Transaction view lists transactions "within a statement period, up until the prior day's close" and is limited to the last 24 months. The Disbursements tab carries a trace ID [official, Amazon staff Quick Tips, via `04-job-money.md` §1]. GitHub discussion #4015 cites the Transaction view help as `sellercentral.amazon.com/gp/help/200141650`.
- **DD+7 definition (Amazon's email, quoted by sellers).** "On March 12, 2026 … we'll update your reserve settings to the standard reserve period of seven days after delivery date (DD+7). Most Amazon sellers worldwide already use DD+7. … if you sell a product on January 1 and it's delivered on January 3, then under the DD+7 policy, your funds for that order will become available for disbursement on January 11. When you ship an order, funds are collected and added to your deferred transaction amount. Upon delivery, funds become reserves … After seven days, the funds are moved into your account balance, which can be disbursed automatically through your choice of the automatic settlement cycle or a once daily disburse-on-demand request. The move to DD+7 may cause a one-time cash flow impact". [official text quoted by sellers] DD+7 March 12, 2026 (2025-09-29), https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/cd0dfc4d-fbc5-4ded-a0b1-4c7ed989c107; same text at …/afacf90a-3e68-4d8d-87aa-8e66d3c24500, read 2026-11-09. Amazon's English examples count the release on the eighth calendar day after delivery: Jan 3 → Jan 11 here, and Jan 6 → Jan 14 in the staff post cited by the survey. Amazon's Chinese explainer (below) is not consistent with them or with itself. It gives "5月3日签收 … 5月10日资金即被释放" (delivered May 3, released May 10, the seventh day) and, with tracking, "5月5日实际签收 → 5月6日起算7天 → 5月13日资金释放" (delivered May 5, count from May 6, released May 13, the eighth day) [command-verified: arithmetic on Amazon's examples]. The release date of a given transaction is the `maturityDate` Amazon returns, not a count.
- **DD+7 as Amazon's Chinese seller site states it** (详解｜亚马逊DD+7预留金政策：底层逻辑、新旧变化和实操建议, 2026-06-05, https://globalselling.amazon.com/news/news-notices-260605, read 2026-11-09) [official]:
  - Stages: buyer pays (授权冻结); seller ships → "进入延迟交易状态" (deferred); delivered → "转入当前预留金" (current reserve); seven days later → withdrawable by Disburse on Demand or the next 14-day automatic settlement.
  - Delivery date: with valid tracking, the actual delivery date, where "追踪号需被亚马逊记录到至少一次物理承运商扫描才算有效" (tracking counts only after at least one physical carrier scan). Without it, "系统使用最迟预计送达日期 (EDD)，EDD = 处理时间 + 运输时间" (the latest estimated delivery date, handling plus transit time).
  - The cycle: "DD+7是在原有结算周期基础上新增的储备金机制，自动结算的14天周期不变" (the 14-day automatic cycle is unchanged). And: "每次通过按需提现提款后，当前结算周期会被"截断"并重新开始计算14天" (each Disburse on Demand truncates the current settlement period and restarts the 14 days). So settlement periods are not all 14 days long [seat, from that sentence].
  - The payment button: "需点击"标准订单"或"发票支付订单"行的"请求付款"按钮。"延迟交易"行的"请求付款"按钮不可用" (request payment on the Standard Orders or Invoiced Orders row; the Deferred transactions row's button is unavailable). DoD can be used once per 24 hours. It is greyed out when nothing is released, when refunds, fees, chargebacks or claims exceed available funds, or when the last request was under 24 hours ago.
  - Migration: "此前已存在于账户级别的预留金额中的DD+7预留金不会迁移到延迟交易" (DD+7 reserves already in the account-level reserve are not moved into deferred transactions).
  - Reports: the new Reports Repository reports show per transaction whether it is deferred or released, and its release date. On business reports not matching payment reports, Amazon recommends the repository's reports.
  - Express Payout: US only; Professional account in good standing, eligible US bank account or eligible Visa/Mastercard debit card, active 90 days or more, no active policy violation. "单笔不超过100万美元，且不支持部分提款" (at most USD 1 million per payout, no partial withdrawal).
  - The article states no transition date. Sellers' threads and cifnews (below) give 2026-03-12 for the remaining accounts, matching the email above.

A single report can provide up to 10 years of data. … You must request the Deferred Transaction Report through the **Payments Reports Repository** on Seller Central before you can use the Reports v2021-06-30 API to retrieve the Deferred Transaction Report." ### E. Payout cycle and disbursement

- "In general, Amazon settles seller accounts every two weeks". Deposits can "take up to five business days for the funds to appear in your bank account". Express Payout is for eligible US sellers, with "payouts within 24 hours" [official] How Amazon seller payments work, https://sell.amazon.com/blog/amazon-seller-payments (2025-07-30), via survey §1/§4.1.
- `FundTransferDate`: "The date and time when the disbursement or charge was initiated. Only present for closed settlements." `TraceId`: "The trace identifier used by sellers to look up transactions externally." `AccountTail`: "The account tail of the payment instrument." `ConvertedTotal`: "The total amount in the currency of the marketplace in which the funds were disbursed." `OriginalTotal`: "in the currency of the marketplace in which the transactions occurred" [official] financesV0.json [command-verified].
- Transfers `Payout.traceId`: "The banking network's unique identifier for a fund transfer request". `paymentRail`: "The underlying payment network or clearing system used to transmit the payout". `PayoutStatusName`: `STARTED`, `IN_TRANSIT`, `PAID`, `FAILED`, `CANCELLED`, `NO_PAYOUT`. `PayoutTypeName`: `SCHEDULED`, `ON_DEMAND`. `PayoutStatusEntry.failureReason`. `ExpectedPayoutStatusName`: `SCHEDULED`, `DISABLED`, `AT_RISK`, `NO_PAYOUT_EXPECTED`. `relatedIdentifiers` enum: `SETTLEMENT_ID` only [official] transfers_2024-06-01.json [command-verified].

### F. Time zones Amazon states

- Settlement V2: `posted-date-time` is "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS **UTC**" (changelog, §A).
- Date Range reports: "12:00 a.m. … 11:59 p.m. **local time**" (§C).
- `listSummary`: "Financial summaries are calculated based on the **marketplace timezone** of the seller, not UTC." [official] Retrieve a financial summary, https://developer-docs.amazon/sp-api/docs/retrieve-financial-summary.md (updatedAt 2026-09-09).
- `listBalances.lastUpdatedTime`: "The value is in the marketplace's local time zone." [official] finances_2024-06-19.json.
- The same day's activity therefore falls under different calendar dates in the settlement file (UTC), the Date Range report and `listSummary` (local) [seat, from the four quotes].

### G. Money Amazon bills or pays outside the settlement (gap fill 2026-11-10)

A settlement holds only what moves through the seller account's balance. Amazon's own pages name other paths, each with its own document.

- **Advertising.** The 2026-08-01 change is above (dated changes; money-flow G4). Amazon Ads: "With Pay by Invoice, we send an invoice at the end of each month, and payment is due 30 days later." "The existing payment method (such as a credit or debit card) will be retained as a backup payment method to ensure uninterrupted service in case of insufficient funds." "This update applies only to the small group of advertisers who have been contacted directly." [official] Update on advertiser payments, https://advertising.amazon.com/library/news/update-on-advertiser-payments, re-read 2026-11-10 (curl). Card and invoice billing therefore remain paths outside the settlement, and the card stays on file even for balance-deducted advertisers [seat, from those sentences].

### I. Regions, accounts and the money operations (gap fill 2026-11-10)

- Every money operation above is called on the regional endpoint of the account's region (NA, EU, FE) with that account's refresh token (§Amazon H). A seller with NA, EU and FE accounts has at least three authorizations, and each additional merged or separate account is another [seat, from Amazon's "Authorize app … for each selling partner account"].
- Within one authorization, `listBalances`, `listSummary`, `listPayouts` and `listExpectedPayouts` take `marketplaceIds` and `accountType`, and return all applicable stores when they are omitted. `listTransactions` takes one `marketplaceId`, and Amazon notes that US MFN calls should not supply it (§B) [command-verified].
- The settlement file carries one `currency` and one `total-amount` per file, with `marketplace-name` per row and empty on account-level rows (GitHub #5291, §What practitioners add).

**What practitioners say breaks reconciliation** (collected; each is named above):
1. the time zone (UTC settlement vs local reports);
2. the V1→V2 switch (row shape, locale-formatted amounts);
3. deferred lines (missing from settlement until released; split across payouts; 2024 and 2026 report changes; 1099-K mismatch);
4. reserve lines (current vs previous, asset not expense, per marketplace);
5. negative settlements and card charges (pending balance, "Successful Charge");
6. account-level rows with no order-id, sku or marketplace (storage, subscription, reimbursements);
7. `FBA Inventory Reimbursement` and other `Adjustment` rows with no order;
8. chargebacks and A-to-z reversals posting cycles later;
9. a settlement crossing month-end;
10. currency of deposit ≠ marketplace currency;
11. failed transfers re-sent next cycle;
12. the 90-day API window;
13. settlement periods shortened by a Disburse on Demand (Amazon China, 2026-06-05; Sellfox says the opposite);
14. advertising charges moved into the settlement for advertisers switched off card billing (from 2026-08-01);
15. DD+7 reserves held in the account-level reserve before migration not moving into deferred transactions (Amazon China; Sellfox).
16. charges billed outside the settlement: ads on card or invoice, Transparency invoices, AGL paid in CNY by wire, Amazon Freight and Amazon Shipping invoices, Lending by Direct Debit (gap fill 2026-11-10);
17. several stores and two account types under one login, account-level rows with no store, and a subscription split across stores;
18. shipped-not-settled and settled-not-shipped orders at month-end (Lingxing);
19. settlements archived in Seller Central, and report documents gone from the API after 90 days.

## Published research

None found. No peer-reviewed or institutional study of marketplace settlement reconciliation surfaced in this session's searches. The ecosystem's knowledge is vendor documentation and Amazon's own posts.

## Stable enough for a skill

1. Settlement reports are made by Amazon on the payout cycle. They are found with `getReports` and never created. Only `…_FLAT_FILE_V2` remains after 2026-11-11.
2. V2 is long-format: one amount per row, classified by `amount-type` + `amount-description`. Amazon publishes no closed list of either.
3. V2 amounts are in the marketplace's locale number format. `posted-date-time` is UTC.
4. Through the API, a settlement report is findable for about 90 days. Older ones are only in Seller Central.
5. The settlement file counts released money in a payout period. It equals the deposit, plus or minus reserve movement and charges. It is not the month's sales.
6. Deferred transactions are absent from settlement files and from v0 event lists until released. They are present in `listTransactions` (`DEFERRED`).
7. Date Range reports run midnight-to-midnight local time on posted date. Since 2026 they include deferred and released (periods from 2025-01-01). No SP-API report type produces them.
8. The Deferred Transaction report is retrievable by API only after the seller requests it in Seller Central.
9. `listSummary` gives net proceeds and breakdowns for a date range (local time) or a settlement id. `listBalances` gives AVAILABLE / DEFERRED / RESERVED / ACCOUNT_LEVEL_RESERVE / TOTAL, now or as of a date.
10. A financial event group is a settlement period. Closed = paid or charged (`OriginalTotal`, `ConvertedTotal`, `FundTransferDate`, `TraceId`, `AccountTail`). Open = current balance.
11. Account-level rows carry no order-id, sku or marketplace. Some fee rows carry an FBA shipment id in `order-id` (practitioner-observed).
12. Remittance and Invoices (2026) are vendor APIs.
13. Accounting choices sellers make (state, don't pick): cash vs accrual; settlement-as-journal vs per-order; gross vs net; clearing account; reserve as asset; deferral accrual; month-split of a settlement; tax as pass-through.
14. Several Amazon charges never pass through a settlement: advertising billed to a card or by invoice; Lending repaid by Direct Debit (UK); AGL paid by wire in CNY; Amazon Freight and Amazon Shipping invoices; Transparency invoices (per a seller and Amazon staff). Each has its own statement outside Seller Central's payments pages.
15. `ProductAdsPaymentEvent.invoiceId` ties an ad line in the settlement to its Ads invoice, and `AdjustmentItem.TransactionNumber` can carry an Amazon Shipping invoice number.
16. The NA, EU and FE regions each have their own SP-API endpoint, and every selling account, merged or not, is authorized separately with its own refresh token. Private applications have a self-authorization limit.
17. Moved to What cx must read at run time (correction 1, 2026-11-10): how a merged account's subscription is split and charged by store is a fee and currency rule that varies by store.
18. Payouts, balances and summaries are kept per store and per account type (Standard Orders, Invoiced Orders).
19. The completeness faces are the Amazon Fulfilled Shipments report (Pricing reaches it), the All Orders reports, the FBA Returns report, `searchOrders` and `listTransactions` by order. Seller-fulfilled returns need a role the seller has not ticked.
20. Each face has its own horizon (§Amazon J). After it, only the seller's own copy or a longer Seller Central document remains.

## What only Seller Central shows

- **Date Range Transaction and Summary reports** (CSV, PDF). No SP-API type exists. The closest API equivalents are `listTransactions` (transactions) and `listSummary` (summary) [official pages above; the equivalence is the reading of the GitHub answer in discussion #4015 (stefnats, 2026-03-18), opinion].
- **Requesting the Deferred Transaction report.** The API only retrieves it afterwards.
- **Settlement history beyond about 90 days** (All statements / Reports Hub "Understand payout"). The API cannot list them.
- **Statement view as a page** (now under Finance → Manage payments in new Seller Central). `listSummary` with `SETTLEMENT_ID` returns the same categories as data.
- **Request Payment / disburse-on-demand** outside the EU8 (`initiatePayout` is EU-only), changing deposit method or settlement cycle, the Currency conversion (ACCS) settings, and the 1099-K / tax document library (tax roles are a sibling's).
- **The reserve reason** ("View details" on the unavailable/reserved balance). No operation returns why a reserve is held [seat: none of the models carries a reason field for `ACCOUNT_LEVEL_RESERVE`].
- **Charges billed outside Seller Central's payments pages** (gap fill 2026-11-10): Ads invoices and billing history (Amazon Ads console; the Ads API is another seat's), Shipper Central invoices (Amazon Shipping; AGL paid in CNY), the Amazon Freight portal, Transparency invoices, and Lending statements. - **Amazon's VAT invoices for its fees** (Tax Document Library), typically monthly per Amazon staff.
- **Re-requesting an archived settlement** ("Request Report", Finance > Finance Reports > Understand Payout, per A2X). The API can find it only afterwards (practitioners).
- **Global Snapshot**, Seller Central's cross-store view. No Amazon page about it was read, and no API reproduces it.

## Facts for human readers only

- Survey and vendors put a typical settlement at 14 days (sometimes 7 for large accounts, per Nova). The bank transfer takes up to 5 business days (Amazon, 2025-07-30). Express Payout is within 24 hours for eligible US sellers (Amazon); the claim that it is on by default from 2026-09-21 is a practitioner's.
- Per A2X (vendor, 2025-08-18), 20–25% of revenue can slip into the next month under DD+7.
- Per Lingxing (vendor), the reserve is 5% of the last 28 days' sales. Amazon publishes no figure.
- Practitioners put typical account-level reserve periods at 14–90 days (A2X, Synder, beancount).
- Per Nova (vendor), account-level fee rows dropped by filtering on SKU are worth 1–3 margin points.
- Subscription across merged accounts (News_Amazon, effective 2021-08-01): total "limited to no more than the equivalent USD 39.99 per month (exclusive of tax, except in Mexico and Brazil, and any foreign transaction fees charged by your bank or credit issuer)". MX changed its subscription on 2026-02-17 (selling-fees).
- Amazon Ads Pay by Invoice: invoice at month-end, "payment is due 30 days later" (Amazon Ads, 2026-04-14). The consolidated invoice statement covers the last 90 days (2024-01-12).
- Amazon Fulfilled Shipments report: at most 12 requests per 8 hours per selling account; 1–3 hours' lag, rarely 24 (FBA Reports, 2026-10-13).
- Authorizations: private apps allow at most 10 self-authorizations (Authorization Limits, 2026-09-09). Authorizations not renewed in more than 365 days and with no call in that time are deactivated (FAQ, 2026-10-29).
- Lingxing (vendor): over 99% of shipped orders settle within three months of shipment, and its shipment-report feed runs about three days late.
- Connector history limits (vendors): Link My Books imports 3 months on connection, up to 24 months for Amazon (2026-10-05); ConnectBooks up to two years; A2X about 10 settlements automatically, then on request.
- Date Range reports: up to 365 days per report, back to 2012 (Amazon staff, 2024-10-31).

## Positions

- **P-rec-1.** Settlement reports cannot be requested or scheduled. Amazon generates them, and cx finds them with `getReports` filtered by `reportTypes=GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_FLAT_FILE_V2`, then fetches each through `getReportDocument`. Source: Settlement Reports (Amazon, 2026-10-14); reports_2021-06-30.json.
- **P-rec-2.** `GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_FLAT_FILE` and `GET_V2_SETTLEMENT_REPORT_DATA_XML` are removed on 2026-11-11 (deprecated 2025-03-17; the removal date moved from 2026-03-25). Calls fail from that date. `…_FLAT_FILE_V2` is the only settlement report, and there is no XML replacement. Source: SP-API Deprecation Schedule; changelog "Deprecation of two payment settlement report types".
- **P-rec-3.** The V2 settlement report is tab-delimited with 24 named columns (list in this file). Amazon publishes the names but no per-column or per-transaction-type definition. Source: Settlement Reports; Amazon-side acknowledgement in GitHub #5353 is [opinion].
- **P-rec-4.** In V2 every amount sits in one `amount` column, classified by `amount-type` and `amount-description`. New fee types arrive as new values, not new columns, and Amazon publishes no closed list of either. Source: Settlement Reports; deprecation changelog.
- **P-rec-5.** V2 amounts are written in the marketplace's local number format (Amazon's example: `95,00` for EU instead of `95.00`). `posted-date` is MM/DD/YY and `posted-date-time` is "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS UTC" (Amazon's changelog). Source: Settlement Reports; deprecation changelog.
- **P-rec-6.** Reports are retained for at most 90 days, and `getReports` looks back 90 days by default, so the API cannot list a settlement report older than about 90 days. Older settlements are downloadable only in Seller Central. Source: reports_2021-06-30.json; Improvado (opinion) agrees.
- **P-rec-7.** Settlement reports require Finance and Accounting. So do `listTransactions`, `listSummary`, `listBalances`, `listFinancialEventsByOrderId`, `listPayouts` and `listExpectedPayouts`. `listFinancialEventGroups` and `listFinancialEventsByGroupId` take Finance and Accounting or, in the EU only, Account Information Service Provider. The Deferred Transaction report is Finance and Accounting per the Payment Reports page and is absent from Role Mappings for Types. Source: Role Mappings for SP-API Operations (2026-10-28); Role Mappings for SP-API Types (2026-09-09); Payment Reports.
- **P-rec-8.** A settlement covers released money in one payout period. Deferred transactions are not in a settlement until they are released, and only disbursed transactions enter settlement reports. Source: News_Amazon 2021-09-20 and 2024-10-04; A2X (opinion).
- **P-rec-9.** Finances v0 event operations (`listFinancialEvents`, `…ByGroupId`, `…ByOrderId`) do not show deferred events until they are released. `listTransactions` (2024-06-19) returns `DEFERRED`, `RELEASED` and `DEFERRED_RELEASED` unless filtered by `transactionStatus`. Source: financesV0.json; finances_2024-06-19.json; Get the latest transactions.
- **P-rec-10.** A financial event group is a settlement period. Its `ProcessingStatus` is `Open` (`OriginalTotal` is then the current balance) or `Closed` (`OriginalTotal` is then the disbursement *or charge* amount). It carries `FundTransferDate` (closed only), `TraceId`, `AccountTail`, `BeginningBalance` and `ConvertedTotal` (in the disbursed currency). Source: financesV0.json; Retrieve the amount and status of a payment; Retrieve your current balance.
- **P-rec-11.** Amazon's documented way to list the transactions behind a payment: take the payment's `FinancialEventGroupId` from `listFinancialEventGroups`, then call `listTransactions` with `relatedIdentifierName=FINANCIAL_EVENT_GROUP_ID` and `transactionStatus=RELEASED`. Source: Determine payment transactions (2026-09-09).
- **P-rec-12.** `listTransactions` filters by `FINANCIAL_EVENT_GROUP_ID` or `ORDER_ID` only: these are "the only `relatedIdentifier` with filtering capabilities at the moment", per the model and the live reference. The 2026-07-29 release note announces a `SETTLEMENT_ID` filter. It was in the model for one day (2026-10-28) and was removed by the 2026-10-29 "Finance API Rollback". `SETTLEMENT_ID` is returned in responses and filters `listSummary`. Source: finances_2024-06-19.json at db2936aa; listTransactions reference (2026-09-10); release notes 2026-07-29; commits df7c6298 and db2936aa [command-verified].
- **P-rec-13.** `listSummary` returns `netProceeds` and nested `breakdowns` for a date range (ISO dates) or for one settlement (`relatedIdentifierName=SETTLEMENT_ID`). Amazon's reference example uses the Statement view's categories (Sales, Product Charges, Expenses, FBA fees, Refunds). Source: finances_2024-06-19.json; listSummary reference; Retrieve a financial summary.
- **P-rec-14.** `listBalances` returns `AVAILABLE`, `RESERVED`, `TOTAL`, `DEFERRED` and `ACCOUNT_LEVEL_RESERVE`, by account type and marketplace, either now or as of a past date (`asOfDate`). Source: finances_2024-06-19.json; release notes 2026-07-29.
- **P-rec-15.** Amazon uses different clocks across documents: settlement `posted-date-time` is UTC; Date Range reports run 12:00 a.m. to 11:59 p.m. local time; `listSummary` "is calculated based on the marketplace timezone of the seller, not UTC"; `listBalances.lastUpdatedTime` is in the marketplace's local time. A transaction near midnight can fall on different dates in each. Source: deprecation changelog; Quick Tip: Date Range reports; Retrieve a financial summary; finances_2024-06-19.json.
- **P-rec-16.** Finance windows on the APIs: a `listTransactions` window over 180 days returns empty, not an error, and so does a `listFinancialEventGroups` window over 180 days. `postedBefore` must be at least two minutes in the past. Events from the last 48 hours may be missing. Pages may be empty while `nextToken` continues. `listFinancialEventsByGroupId` covers only the last two years. Source: finances_2024-06-19.json; financesV0.json.
- **P-rec-17.** Date Range Transaction (CSV) and Summary (PDF) reports are requested in Seller Central (Payments/Finance → Reports Repository). They cover transactions by the date posted to the account, "may not correspond to the order date or the shipment date", and can take up to three hours to generate. Source: Quick Tip: Date Range reports (Amazon staff).
- **P-rec-18.** From 2026, for periods from 2025-01-01, Date Range Transaction and Summary reports include deferred and released transactions. Their Date/Time is the posted date, and the Transaction report gains a deferred/released status column and a Transaction Release Date (blank while deferred). The rollout was US/CA/MX/BR by 2026-04-30; BE/FR/DE/IE/IT/NL/PL/ES/SE/IN/EG/SA/TR/AE/UK from 2026-03-23 to 2026-05-31; other stores by 2026-06-30. Periods before 2025-01-01 keep the old format. Source: News_Amazon 2026-03-18 and 2026-03-31.
- **P-rec-19.** No SP-API report type produces the Date Range Transaction or Summary report. The Report Type Values page lists, under Payment, only `GET_DATE_RANGE_FINANCIAL_HOLDS_DATA`. Source: Report Type Values (2026-09-10); Payment Reports.
- **P-rec-20.** `GET_DATE_RANGE_FINANCIAL_HOLDS_DATA` (Deferred Transaction Report) is a point-in-time CSV of currently deferred transactions, covering up to 10 years. The API can retrieve it only after the seller requests it in Seller Central's Payments Reports Repository, and a released transaction leaves it. Source: Payment Reports; changelog "New: Deferred Transaction Report type"; News_Amazon 2024-10-04; Amazon's reply on GitHub #4830.
- **P-rec-21.** Delivery-date reserve (DD+7): funds are deferred at shipment, become reserves on delivery, and move to the balance seven days later. The delivery date is the tracked delivery (at least one physical carrier scan) or, without valid tracking, the latest estimated delivery date (handling plus transit time). Amazon's worked examples disagree on the day count: the English ones land on the eighth calendar day after delivery, and one Chinese one on the seventh. A transaction's own release date is its `maturityDate`. Invoiced (B2B) orders are also deferred, and `DeferredContext.deferralReason` names `DD7` or `B2B`. Amazon moved remaining accounts to DD+7 on the date its e-mail gave each account: a US e-mail gives 2026-03-12, and UK/EU sellers report e-mails giving 2026-03-05, 2026-03-12 or 2026-03-19 (EU forum thread 7d30d954, 2025-09-30). No Amazon page gives these dates; they are known only from e-mails sellers pasted in Seller Forums. Source: Amazon's email quoted in Seller Forums (US, 2025-09-29); Amazon's e-mails as UK/EU sellers posted them, "DD+7 Deferred Again" (https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/7d30d954-dd74-4c46-b01e-48a7cc7f5c45, 2025-09-30, via money-flow P-flow-14); Amazon China DD+7 explainer (2026-06-05); finances_2024-06-19.json; Amazon staff post via survey. Read at run time: the reserve period that applies to the seller's own marketplace is read per transaction from `listTransactions` (`DeferredContext.deferralReason` and `maturityDate`, against `postedDate`), and the deferred total from `listBalances` (`DEFERRED`).
- **P-rec-22.** An account-level reserve is distinct from a deferred transaction. It can remain for a claim, a chargeback or an account review.
