# Published measures of Amazon's take and of fee pass-through

## Amazon's total take

- Marketplace Pulse (2023-02-13; private label, 2024-03-18), ILSR (H1 2023, advocacy, 2023-09-21) and the FTC complaint (2023-09-26, an allegation) each measure Amazon's total take from a typical third-party seller's revenue; research/beyond/sellers-tools-and-research.md, P-tool-23, holds the values as read. (P-tool-23)
- Each of these measures splits the take into referral, FBA (including storage) and advertising. (P-tool-23)

## Who bears a fee change

- Published studies find that sellers pass part, not all, of an Amazon fee change into price: fee incidence on Amazon (Tsai, 2021), referral and fulfilment fee pass-through (Gutiérrez, 2022), and digital service tax pass-through (Langenmayr and Muddasani, CESifo 12713, 2026). (P-prof-39)
- Tsai's working paper (October 2021) concludes that "sellers bear more of the burden of platform fees than consumers"; its estimates are in research/beyond/sellers-tools-and-research.md, P-tool-24. (P-tool-24)
- A 2026 CESifo paper (Langenmayr and Muddasani, seen only as a snippet, unverified) reports that Amazon raised fees by about each country's digital services tax. (P-tool-24)
