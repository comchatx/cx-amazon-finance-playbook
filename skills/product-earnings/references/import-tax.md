# Import VAT, GST and consumption tax in a product's cost

## In or out of landed cost

- Import VAT, GST and import consumption tax are recoverable by a registered importer in the regimes read. (P-prof-50)
- Practitioners keep them out of landed cost for such a seller and in it for a seller who cannot recover them, while customs duty stays in cost: "Import VAT … Reclaimable as input tax, so not a cost of the goods"; "If you cannot reclaim import VAT, it becomes a real, non-recoverable cost" (Zmartly). (P-prof-50)
- IAS 2 counts in purchase cost "import duties and other taxes (other than those subsequently recoverable …)" (seen as a snippet). (P-prof-50)
- The rule for each import country is on its tax authority's page: GOV.UK *Check when you can account for import VAT on your VAT Return* (UK), NTA *No.6563 輸入取引* (import transactions; JP), CRA *GST/HST on imports and exports* (CA), Belastingdienst *Reverse-charging VAT when doing business abroad* (NL), ATO *What is the deferred GST scheme?* (AU, seen only as snippets); no operation or page read gives another country's. (P-prof-50)

## United Kingdom

- UK postponed VAT accounting lets a UK-VAT-registered importer declare and recover import VAT on the same VAT return, with no payment at the border and no approval. (P-prof-51)
- It applies to goods used in the importer's business that it has the right to dispose of (usually as owner), with its VAT number on the import declaration. (P-prof-51)
- A non-established business needs someone to deal with customs who enters it as consignee, and a person importing for it needs written instructions. (P-prof-51)
- The choice cannot be changed after the declaration. (P-prof-51)
- The monthly postponed import VAT statement is on HMRC's Customs Declaration Service, and VAT paid at the border is evidenced by a C79 (GOV.UK *Get your postponed import VAT statement*). (P-prof-51)

## Japan

- In Japan the import consumption tax is owed by whoever takes the goods out of the bonded area, that is the import declarant. (P-prof-52)
- It is charged on CIF value plus duty and other excise, even for unregistered persons. (P-prof-52)
- The input tax credit belongs to the declarant with authority to dispose of the goods after import, not to an agent who arranged clearance and paid the tax temporarily (an NTA question-and-answer example, 質疑応答事例). (P-prof-52)
- The NTA states that a foreign seller declaring imports must appoint a customs affairs administrator (税関事務管理人). (P-prof-52)

## Canada, the Netherlands, Australia

- In Canada the owner or importer of record pays GST/HST at import, and a GST/HST-registered importer may claim it as an input tax credit. (P-prof-53)
- In the Netherlands an Article 23 licence moves import VAT from customs to the VAT return. (P-prof-53)
- Australia's deferred GST scheme moves it to a monthly BAS (ATO, seen only as snippets). (P-prof-53)

## Who Amazon names as importer

- In Remote Fulfillment with FBA (US stock sold into Canada, Mexico and Brazil), customers are the importer and pay the import duties and taxes. (P-prof-54)
- Between the UK and the EU, Local Inbounding has the seller as importer with a VAT number in the destination, and Remote Fulfillment has the customer as importer, with Amazon collecting and remitting VAT. (P-prof-54)
- Amazon Global Logistics asks the seller for importer-of-record details and supplies brokers. (P-prof-54)
- No Amazon page read says Amazon is the importer for a seller's inbound FBA stock. (P-prof-54)

## What practitioners add

- When a forwarder clears goods duty-paid under its own VAT number (DDP, called 包税 in Chinese), the seller is not the importer and has historically not reclaimed the import VAT (a seller on Seller Forums EU). (P-prof-55)
- Amazon settlements can carry import VAT and customs duty as one combined line, with the split on the shipment's customs entry document (Link My Books). (P-prof-55)
- Practitioners name EU states besides the UK that allow or refuse import VAT deferral; no operation or page read gives the rule for France, Ireland, Belgium or Italy, and research/areas/product-profit.md, P-prof-55, holds it as read. (P-prof-55)
