# Recovery services and how seller tools book reimbursements

## Paid recovery services

- Paid recovery services (GETIDA, Seller Investigators, Helium 10 Managed Refund Service, SELLERLOGIC, Refund Retriever) take a share of what they recover and file through Seller Central. (P-ref-31)
- Threecolts Margin Pro and Seller 365 also charge a share of what they recover; the vendors' pages state look-backs, and none states how a claim's value relates to the seller's COGS. (P-tool-17)
- Each vendor's share and look-back are read from its own page; no SP-API operation or Amazon page gives them, and research/beyond/sellers-tools-and-research.md, P-tool-17, holds them as read. (P-tool-17)
- Several vendor pages still describe superseded rules: long audit look-backs, "no proactive reimbursement", and the superseded 2025-03-10 valuation date. (P-ref-31)
- GETIDA reports, from its own sample, that Amazon pays almost all FBA customer-return issues on its own; research/areas/refunds-reimbursements-adjustments.md, § Facts for human readers only, holds its figure as read. (P-ref-31)
- No vendor's "share of revenue owed" figure cites a method. (P-ref-31)
- SPS Commerce, which bought Carbon6 (Seller Investigators) in 2025, sold its 3P revenue recovery business on 2026-06-30 and named Amazon's 3P policy changes as a reason on its earnings call. (P-ref-32)

## Chinese ERPs

- Chinese ERPs estimate what Amazon owes and track the Case ID the seller enters against the Reimbursements report; none files claims. (P-ref-39)
- For valuation, Gerpgo uses the latest reimbursed unit price; Lingxing and Sellfox use the fee difference times quantity. (P-ref-39)
- Lingxing nets reversals in one reimbursement line and puts A-to-z and SAFE-T money under other income. (P-ref-39)
- Gerpgo offsets the reimbursed units' purchase and freight cost. (P-ref-39)
- ECCANG lets the seller count each reimbursement kind in or out of profit. (P-ref-39)
- Which treatment applies is the seller's choice. (P-ref-39)
