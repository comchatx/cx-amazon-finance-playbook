# The seller's own imports

## Importer of record

- Amazon does not act as importer of record for FBA inventory "regardless of the shipment size or value". (P-tax-45)
- The seller or its agent is the importer of record and pays duties and import taxes. (P-tax-45)
- With Amazon Global Logistics, the seller still needs an approved importer of record per import country and signs a POA for AGL to clear customs. (P-tax-45)

## Import VAT

- Import VAT paid by a VAT-registered importer is recoverable under the normal input-tax rules. (P-tax-46)
- UK postponed VAT accounting declares and recovers import VAT on the same VAT return, with monthly statements on CDS. (P-tax-46)
- EU states apply postponed accounting, deferred payment, or both. (P-tax-46)
- Customs duty is a figure separate from import VAT. (P-tax-46)

## Moves of own goods inside Europe

- A Pan-EU or EFN move of own stock across a border is a VAT event in both countries; references/eu-uk.md holds the rule. (P-tax-47)
