# How deferred and reserved money is booked

## Deferred and reserved money

- How a seller books deferred and reserved money is the seller's choice, and practitioners use three treatments. (P-flow-32)
- One treatment accrues deferred sales in the month earned and reverses them on release (A2X, Link My Books, Bookkeep). (P-flow-32)
- One carries reserves as a current asset or receivable that nets to zero (A2X, Link My Books, Synder). (P-flow-32)
- One posts reserves as expense and deposit (Webgility). (P-flow-32)
- Each treatment changes month-end figures differently. (P-flow-32)
- Lingxing offers profit on a settlement-time basis and on a ship-time basis. (P-flow-32)

## Month-spanning settlements and FX

- A settlement can span two calendar months. (P-flow-33)
- A2X and Link My Books split a settlement that spans two months and carry a balance between the two halves; booking it whole on its period, which puts every line in one month, is the other choice, and the choice is the seller's. (P-flow-33, P-rec-32)
- Lingxing values a settlement's FX at the month-start rate of the month the settlement began. (P-flow-33)
- These splitting and FX methods are tools' methods, not Amazon rules. (P-flow-33)
