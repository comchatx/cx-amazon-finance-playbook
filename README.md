cx ties each Amazon payout to your bank and works out what each product earned after Amazon's fees and your own costs. It reads your transactions, fees, refunds, reimbursements and settlements through the Amazon SP-API plugin, in each marketplace you connected there.

Some things you can ask:

- Why was my last payout so much lower than my sales?
- What did B0EXAMPLE1 earn last month after fees and my unit cost?
- Which lines on my German settlement carry VAT, and who pays it?

What your ads earn against their cost is the Sponsored Ads Playbook's, and your stock and restocking are the Stock Playbook's.

## What it needs

- **Amazon SP-API:** your Amazon selling account, connected on the Amazon SP-API plugin. cx reads your money there.
- **Amazon Ads,** where you have it: each product's ad spend, where Amazon's own per-product figures are not open to you.

Each is its own plugin, and its page gives its price.
